# Product

What the product does: behaviour, journeys, wording and errors, grouped by feature (§1–15), admin page (§16) and public page (§17). Data invariants and calculations are in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), visual and interaction rules and page approvals in [UI.md](UI.md), production operations in [OPERATIONS.md](OPERATIONS.md).

## 1. Product, scope and scale

- DK Legacy is a private website that runs Old School RuneScape bingo events for one Discord community, normally two or three events a year. It replaces the Google Sheets board and the Discord evidence workflow; Discord stays the community's communication platform.
- An event may include invited outside teams (for example another clan) that bring an internally selected roster and need not take part in the website draft.
- The website manages public boards and live team progress; teams, rosters, captains and co-captains; evidence submission and Admin review; rankings, placements and contribution statistics; board creation and EHB balancing; the team draft; and historical events and evidence. It is not a reusable platform for other communities.
- Goals: every team's official progress is easy to follow in one place; drop submission is fast for captains and co-captains; Admins get a clear review queue and safe correction tools; tile, line, board and ranking progress is calculated automatically; the varied drop objectives of real OSRS boards are supported; previous events, results, rosters, boards and approved evidence are preserved; organizers can estimate and balance boards with EHB; the team draft runs on the website under Admin control.
- Representative event: about 6 teams × 14 players (about 84 participants), usually a captain and co-captain per team, a 5×5 or 6×6 board, from an extended weekend to about six days. The common size is 3–5 teams. Simplicity and reliability matter more than internet-scale optimization.

## 2. Roles and permissions

### Identity and participation

- The website account is the durable identity: username and password after onboarding, optional Discord link. Participation and OSRS characters are separate records.
- Event participation is explicit ownership (`EventParticipant.AccountId`), one participation per account per event. It is never inferred from a Discord name, username, OSRS name, volunteer answer or emergency credential.
- A character may be linked to several accounts; within one event each character is assigned to exactly one participant. Authority or ownership is never inferred from a character name.
- New participation requires an existing active website account created by its user. There is no accountless or external participant creation, no application CSV roster import and no participant-owner transfer; the separate operator historical import (§19) remains.

### Public visitors

- Without an account, visitors can read the current event, the How To guide, dates and status; the signup table with its public fields before draft finalization (afterwards non-Admins are redirected to the published rosters); published boards and approved progress; tile details and public approved screenshots; teams, rosters and draft results; leaderboards; and history.
- They cannot submit, see pending or rejected evidence, or change anything.

### Participants

- A signed-in participant manages their own signup while it is open, sees their event and team access, submits for themselves when allowed, and opens the `/Submissions` ledger with the complete retained history of their current team, including departed teammates' records.
- Participants see their own private signup and evidence; Captains see their current team's scope; Admins see event administration.

### Captains and co-captains

- Captain and co-captain are event/team roles on an existing account-linked membership, not tied to a character, Discord ID or sign-in method. Assigning or removing the role changes permissions with no separate login. Roles differ per event, follow the authoritative roster role, are scoped to one event and team, and are unchanged by character changes or swaps. The submission cutoff restricts actions, not identity.
- Captain and co-captain have identical permissions. They can:
  - see the confirmed draft-pool signup table with answers expanded before and during the draft (excluding paid/unpaid, private notes, recovery/security data and audit); access follows the current team role and draft inclusion, and a legacy formation type grants nothing;
  - submit for their own team through the same team-board drawer and credit a teammate;
  - view and filter the full team ledger (status, player, tile) with feedback and summary counts, and edit or withdraw the team's pending submissions;
  - set and clear the team focus (tile, row or column).
- They cannot submit for other teams, see other teams' pending or rejected evidence, approve or reverse, change progress, or edit the event, board, catalogue, roster or rules.

### Admins

- Admins use permanent normal accounts and may also participate or captain. The global role is additive: it composes with a genuine event role but never creates or upgrades one. Team navigation, team visibility and submission authority follow the genuine event role within its lifecycle and cutoff; global status grants no team capability. Global review is Admin-only and stays separate from team roles, including for the Admin's own team's submissions.
- Every enabled Admin may create and configure every event. Admins create and configure events; publish results (atomically archiving the event); cancel populated pre-Live events; create teams and manage permitted rosters before first Live; assign and revoke captain roles; manage the catalogue; build boards; operate and publish the draft; review, approve and reject; correct pending metadata; reverse approvals; and view the complete audit log. They submit only through a genuine team role.
- Competitive Admin actions are audited. Admin access lasts until revoked.

### Super Admin and global roles

- Exactly one active Super Admin exists, on a normal account, and inherits Admin. The initial owner is set during controlled setup or migration, never by first signup, login or onboarding. Global roles are independent of participation, Discord and captaincy; any normal account may become Admin.
- Only the Super Admin grants or revokes Admin, transfers ownership and uses owner-only capabilities; ordinary Admins cannot manage global roles. Accounts shows grant/revoke and Transfer only when server policy permits.
- Grant/revoke uses a before/after confirmation with no typed name or reason; revoking returns the account to User. Transfer requires the owner's password and the typed destination username. The owner cannot self-demote except by transfer.
- Every grant, revoke, transfer, disable or restore increments the account's authorization version and invalidates its sessions; a stale session gets an access-changed / sign-in-again outcome. Invalid, stale, zero-owner or multiple-owner outcomes fail atomically. Changes are recorded automatically.
- Disabling is separate from roles: an Admin may disable a `USER`; only the Super Admin disables or restores an `ADMIN`; nobody disables themselves or the Super Admin. Disabling requires a reason (private to Admins and audit) and preserves relationships, the reserved username and the Discord link. A disabled account cannot authenticate and sees neutral contact-an-admin guidance at login, never the reason. Re-enabling is confirmed and audited without a reason.
- Granting or revoking Admin and restoring an account append a personal in-site notification to the target.
- The Super Admin gets no automatic cross-team private focus or evidence authority. Other teams' focus is visible only through an explicit, read-only inspection (§7). Automatic Super Admin captaincy and cross-team submission inspection do not exist.
- Lost-owner recovery is an operator procedure (§3), audited.

### No emergency Captain authority

- There is no emergency Captain login, session, token use, recovery, reset, enable/disable, evidence, Team Focus or worker authority. Retained emergency accounts, tokens and actor references are historical and readable only: never converted, deleted, expired or given fabricated audit. Emergency credentials can never hold a global role.
- Captain authority is membership-based. Live start has no Captain or credential prerequisite; website-draft start and finalization still require current Captains (§7).

## 3. Accounts, sign-in and recovery

### Creating an account

- Every normal account starts with **Continue with Discord** (Discord server membership is not required). The first Discord authentication creates only protected onboarding state valid for 15 minutes; no account exists until the onboarding transaction succeeds. The Discord callback is an immediate non-rendering redirect.
- Onboarding requires a unique public username, a password and a first OSRS character, chosen independently. Values are trimmed and checked for case-insensitive uniqueness without rewriting spelling. The character name must pass the shared name rule and the Wise Old Man account check; this shows the name exists, not who owns it. The username, password hash, character link (position 01, preferred), preferred account and completion commit atomically; a uniqueness conflict gives a field-specific error and never merges accounts.
- OSRS name checks by flow: a typed new or renamed name must be 1–12 letters, digits, spaces, `-` or `_` ("Use up to 12 letters, numbers, spaces, - or _."). Onboarding, My accounts add/rename and public signup also check the name with Wise Old Man. The Admin participant drawer checks the name rule only (no Wise Old Man call; a typed name becomes an event-only account, never a saved account of the player). Admin Add uses the player's saved accounts and EHB. Stored names are never re-validated.
- Neither the username nor the character proves ownership; username uniqueness does not stop others linking the same character. Account and character sharing is trust-based; event assignment uniqueness is authoritative.
- An expired onboarding or reset creates no partial account. Discord onboarding and password change preserve a validated local ReturnUrl.

### Signing in

- Discord and username/password sign-in reach the same account; the public username is the login name. Discord display names are non-authoritative support metadata.
- The standalone Login page (with a validated local ReturnUrl) is the only login surface; there is no login popup.
- Passwords are at least 10 characters; passphrases and any printable characters are allowed; no composition or expiry rules. A normal sign-in ends with the browser session, at most 12 hours; **Remember me** lasts at most 30 days absolute.
- Login and reset feedback is generic and never discloses whether an account exists. Settings offers password change, Link/Change/Unlink Discord and My accounts.

### Username

- A username may change to any valid, case-insensitively unique value, with no dependence on character links. The UI confirms it becomes the login name. The change updates public identity and login name together, reissues only the current session, and never modifies character links, participants, assignments, evidence, teams, roles or history (event-facing names come from OSRS characters).

### Password recovery

- No email is collected. The static Forgot password page asks for no username, does no lookup, and says to contact an Admin.
- After out-of-band verification an enabled Admin generates a reset link for a `USER`; only the Super Admin can for an `ADMIN`; there is no in-product reset for the current Super Admin, who recovers through Discord or the operator procedure. Admins never see or choose the password.
- The token is high-entropy, stored only as a hash, single-use, expires after 60 minutes and supersedes older tokens; the raw link is shown once. Generation and use are recorded automatically, with no reason. A reset invalidates password sessions but not separate Discord sessions, and keeps the same identity.
- Lost-owner recovery is an operator-only CLI, never web: a purpose-bound 60-minute reset link for the owner, or an atomic transfer to an active account, using explicit IDs and a confirmation argument, audited as the system actor.

### Discord link

- Discord can be linked, unlinked or changed at any time, including during events and for privileged accounts, after a fresh password confirmation. Linking or changing goes through a purpose-bound OAuth callback and needs a globally unused Discord ID; a change replaces directly with no unlinked state in between. The Super Admin is warned before unlinking (recovery then depends on the operator) but may proceed.
- Each change is locked, recorded and increments the authorization version atomically; the current browser gets a fresh session and other sessions are invalidated. Participation, roles, characters, evidence and history are untouched.
- Discord IDs and usernames are unique; link/unlink/change and reset are atomic and audited. OAuth tokens, raw passwords and recovery tokens never appear in audit. Legacy Discord free text never infers ownership.

### My accounts (OSRS characters)

- My accounts is the ordered list of the account's globally linked OSRS characters, with optional labels and an optional personal EHB default per link. Position 01 is the preferred character; reordering moves the preference (there is no separate set-preferred action). Labels and order are presentation only.
- Characters may be shared across website accounts but belong to one participant per event. The primary signup account is the initial active character at event start.
- Saved EHB (manual or fetched from Wise Old Man) is stored and shown with at most two decimals, standard rounding (3000.09582 → 3000.10). Event snapshots are unaffected by later My accounts edits.
- Unlinking hides a character from future selection only; it never deletes event assignments or evidence. Unlinking a character with an upcoming or live registration needs explicit confirmation (fails closed without); the registration and history stay.
- A spelling correction (global rename) relinks the entry and editable signups atomically; closed, live and historical records are unchanged; any conflict in an editable event rolls the whole change back.
- Duplicate, unavailable or stale changes fail cleanly. Live account swaps are covered in §6.

### No merging or deletion

- There is no account merge, participant ownership transfer or permanent account deletion; old records of these remain readable. Development identities are removed only by the clean development reset (§20).

## 4. Event lifecycle

### States

- Draft → Signup open → Signup closed → Live → Awaiting final review → Archived, plus Cancelled and Discarded.
  - **Draft**: private configuration.
  - **Signup open**: public signup; teams and board stay private.
  - **Signup closed**: signup ended, draft and setup continue.
  - **Live**: the approved board and finalized teams are public; in-window drops can be submitted.
  - **Awaiting final review**: the window has ended; evidence for in-window drops may be uploaded for 30 minutes; review continues.
  - **Archived**: entered atomically when official results are published (there is no separate Archive action); results and public URLs are immutable; public history.
  - **Finalized**: legacy state, readable only; never a new resting state.
  - **Cancelled**: terminal preserved state for a populated event that never went Live.
  - **Discarded**: terminal tombstone for an empty experimental event.
- Actual transitions are recorded as history. The event never finalizes automatically; publishing results requires Admin confirmation (§13). A reasoned reopening of results returns to Awaiting final review only when current-event exclusivity permits.
- Evidence codes, exceptional upload reopening, current-event exclusivity and quarantine (hiding) apply across states as described below and in §11/§13.

### Readiness and presentation

- Readiness is evaluated separately for saving a draft, opening signup, starting the draft, starting/publishing the event and finalization; each classifies unmet work as a blocker, an overridable warning or a task irrelevant until later.
- Derived presentation phases add no state: "Signups closed", "Draft finalized", and publicly "Board published" with the scheduled start. Admins see "Event ready · Starts …" only with zero start blockers, otherwise "Board published · Not ready", or "Start postponed" for an overdue blocked scheduled start. Labels never disclose blockers publicly and never change routing, authorization or readiness.

### Publication controls

- Separate publication controls exist for the signup page, participant list, draft results and order, team rosters, board and tiles, and results. Opening signup publishes only the signup page and basic event information; captain accounts and Admin configuration are never exposed. Board and draft publication are independent.

### Opening and closing signup

- A future opening timestamp schedules opening (there is no Admin toggle). Manual Open, Close and Reopen each use one confirmation showing the consequence and close time, with readiness rechecked on submit; there is no prepare, warning-acknowledgement or proposed-close step. A manual action supersedes only its matching scheduled transition.
- Manual opening records `actual_signup_opened_at` without overwriting `signup_opens_at`. With no explicit close it sets `signup_closes_at = min(round_up(now + 3 months), event_starts_at)`; a valid explicit future close no later than the start is kept; an invalid explicit close blocks opening and is never silently overwritten. Defaults are previewed and committed only in the successful transaction.
- Reopen reuses the configured close if it is still future, otherwise establishes and confirms a replacement future close; prior transitions are retained. Starting the draft closes and locks signup regardless of the configured close.
- Manual and scheduled opening share the readiness policy; manual opening ignores the stored schedule. Scheduling stores the acknowledged warning codes. At the scheduled instant the scheduler reruns readiness transactionally: a blocker or a new unacknowledged warning keeps signup closed, records one failed attempt, disables retry and creates one visible Admin action showing the blockers. Provider reachability is excluded: temporary Discord or Wise Old Man failures never rewrite state, and participant operations show accurate unavailable/retry feedback.

### Current-event exclusivity

- Several Signup open/closed events may coexist when their half-open windows [start, end) do not overlap (back-to-back is allowed). Signup is an unlisted exact-link journey, not added to navigation or the current-event selector.
- Only Live, Awaiting final review and Finalized are singleton current states. Lifecycle commands, Restore and result reopening lock the current operational event and check exclusivity transactionally; a conflict returns the blocking event and a recovery route and mutates nothing. Public and history routes stay stable.
- Exclusivity is enforced in the application, not by a database constraint, because development scenarios need simultaneous events: development fixtures carry an internal persisted marker honored only by Development commands, never settable by Admin input or honored in Production. Development and test code use explicit IDs and slugs.

### Starting the event

- The scheduler and **Start event now** use the same lifecycle service. At the configured start the server rechecks the finalized draft (or finalized manual rosters), the published board, valid assignments, the schedule, the single current event and all invariants. Live begins at the actual transition time and is never backdated.
- A blocked automatic start leaves the event pre-Live, keeps the configured instant as history, records the attempt and creates or updates one idempotent **Automatic start postponed** action with the exact blockers and direct links to resolve them (Draft, Board, Schedule, Teams). There is no delayed retry into a surprise start.
- Manual start works before, at or after the configured instant, with one strong confirmation and no reason. The scheduled attempt, blocker state, actual start and audit stay distinct.
- Recovery from a postponed start: while the event has not started and its configured end is future, Admins may finish roster corrections, draft finalization and board publication, then start manually without rewriting the missed start. If the configured end has also passed, start fails closed until the event window is repaired to future values or the event is cancelled.

### Ending, early end and Resume

- The submission cutoff is derived as exactly event end + 30 minutes whenever the end is set or changed, unset without an end, never configurable and not ordinarily displayed. A separately approved upload reopening keeps its own cutoff (§11).
- The event ends automatically at the configured end into Awaiting final review; late processing still uses the configured instant as the actual end, and a late worker catches up without extending play.
- An enabled Admin may end a Live event early with strong confirmation and a reason; no lead time is required. The actual end keeps the precise click instant; the configured end and the requested Wise Old Man end become the click instant rounded up to the next whole minute (21:59:55 → 22:00:00; an exact minute is unchanged). The confirmation states that rounded end. Cutoff = actual end + 30 minutes.
- Both scheduled and early end close new-drop eligibility and allow grace uploads until the cutoff, without rewriting evidence, swaps, focus or roster. A screenshot whose timestamp proves a post-end drop fails review; screenshot time is checked visually, with no OCR.
- **Resume** returns an Awaiting final review event (ended manually or automatically) to Live before official finalization. It needs strong confirmation, a reason and a validated future replacement end in schedule increments, always asked even if the retained end is still future, stored as the configured end with no rounding and never the click time. It returns to Live only after singleton and lifecycle checks; the earlier end stays history and the final-review interval and its submissions and reviews remain; the cutoff is re-derived and any separate reopening window revalidated. Membership stays locked. A failed Resume leaves the event unchanged. Resume is unavailable from Finalized, Archived or after official finalization (fails closed).
- Early end and Resume commit locally and record a pending Wise Old Man end update (§14). No five-minute tolerance applies to Wise Old Man linking, replacement, fetch, Schedule save or Resume: matching uses the exact configured UTC start and end.

### Discard and cancel

- **Delete event** (discard) is allowed only while no participants, teams, event-scoped account access, submissions or evidence exist; boards, tiles, requirements, questions and planning configuration are removed with it. It needs confirmation; the server rechecks emptiness immediately before cleanup, and a racing protected record makes it fail. A minimal tombstone keeps the event ID, name, retired slug, creator, discard actor, times and audit; the event leaves all listings and the slug stays reserved.
- **Cancel event** applies to a populated event that never went Live: strong confirmation and a required reason. Cancellation is terminal in the UI, locks the event, closes signup and all ordinary mutations, makes the scheduler ignore it, deletes nothing and has no ordinary resume. A never-public event stays non-public; an already-public one (`first_public_at` set) shows only its prior published projection with a generic cancelled status; the reason stays private.
- Live events use early end and finalization instead.

### Hidden events (quarantine)

- Hiding is a reversible quarantine orthogonal to state. Only the Super Admin hides or restores, from Events Control or the Manage Danger Zone, with ordinary confirmation and an immutable audit entry; Hide needs a reason, Restore none; no typed-name confirmation.
- Only Awaiting final review, Finalized and Archived events can be hidden; Draft, Signup open/closed, Live, Cancelled and Discarded cannot.
- One service owns Hide/Restore (eligibility, confirmation, reason, Super Admin check, optimistic concurrency) and atomically sets or clears `HiddenAt`, `HiddenByAccountId` and `HiddenReason` with audit. It rewrites no competitive data and sends no notifications.
- A hidden event cannot transition or use any workspace and is absent from every public, participant, captain and ordinary-Admin surface: discovery, history, account, submission, evidence, notification, action and realtime projections. Direct URLs return 404, also for the Super Admin, except the separate Hidden area of Events Control, whose only destination is a limited Manage inspection (lifecycle information, hide/restore history, Restore). Ordinary Admins cannot see or operate hidden events.
- Hidden events get no scheduler, signup, collision or realtime processing. Data is retained, not deleted.
- Restore is the only recovery and clears only the hiding metadata. Restoring an event in a current state is rejected, keeping all metadata, if another visible current event exists (same lock and fixture exclusions as Start/Resume); an Archived event restores without claiming the current slot. Invalid state, authority, missing reason or confirmation, or a stale request fails without mutation.

## 5. Event setup

### Creating an event

- Admins start at Events → Create; setup continues through Identity, Schedule, Signup setup, participants and the exact-link signup. The event stays private until signup opens.
- Create asks only for a name (at most 50 Unicode code points) and a timezone (default Europe/Copenhagen). In one transaction it creates a private Draft with a unique event ID, a permanent collision-safe slug, creator/time metadata, the standard signup questions, an empty 5×5 board and safe defaults; no participant, team, captain or evidence is implied. Invalid name or timezone or lost authorization creates nothing. Create returns to the setup workspace; setup spans sessions and any other Admin can continue it.
- The slug is generated from the name, unique and permanent; renaming never changes it. Identity shows the permanent public entry link; there is no slug editor. Duplicate display names are allowed. Creation and setup changes are audited with the actor.
- Retry safety: each create carries a non-empty UUID request key scoped to the Admin (the GET form supplies it and keeps it across validation errors and retries). Repeats with the same actor, key and trimmed name/timezone commit exactly one event, audit and outcome and return the original; the same key with different input is rejected; a missing or malformed key is rejected; invalid requests consume no key; a new key may create a same-named event.
- **Check again** (`/Admin/Events/Create?handler=CheckAgain&requestId=…`) returns only the requesting actor's committed event ID, read by key under current permissions, never by name; absence does not prove failure and a same-key retry stays safe. Later renames do not change the request identity. Hidden outcomes obey Super Admin visibility, discarded ones are unavailable, and neither frees the key. Revoked or disabled Admins cannot create, replay or look up.

### Identity

- Identity owns the name, optional public description, buy-in information (free text) and timezone. Events have no banner, per-event public rules, prize description, expected team count or expected board rows/columns.
- Name, description and buy-in are editable through Live and Awaiting final review. The 50-code-point name limit applies only when the name changes; description and buy-in limits count UTF-16 units.
- Timezone is chosen from the server's canonical set (default Europe/Copenhagen; the UI shows labels and offsets and posts the canonical ID). It changes display only and never moves stored UTC instants. It changes freely while private; after signup opens and before Live, a change reviews all five moments (Signups open, Signups close, Team draft, Event starts, Event ends) with old and new offsets, explicit unscheduled rows and any other fields saved together; the confirmation binds the zones and consequences at stored precision, and a schedule change in the meantime makes the review stale and requires re-confirmation. The timezone locks permanently at first Live.
- Saves compare original, intended and current values per field: untouched fields keep another Admin's changes; a change is safe when current equals original or already equals the intent; a differing same-field edit blocks the whole save until resolved with Keep mine or Use current against the reviewed current value, and a newer same-field change invalidates that resolution. Failure keeps the baseline and the draft; baseline-free or malformed submissions fail closed. Unsupported timezone, slug mutation, unresolved conflict or stale timezone review keeps prior values. Recomparison happens in the write transaction; unchanged values write no mutation or audit.
- Identity changes are Admin-authorized, phase-checked, Serializable, concurrency-protected and audited atomically.

### Schedule

- Schedule owns five timestamps in the event timezone, stored as UTC: signup opening, signup closing, optional team draft time, event start and event end. It does not own capacity (Signup setup) and has no automatic-opening toggle; it never writes capacity or promotes waiters. Opening, closing, reopening and schedule edits are route-backed form actions.
- A private Draft may be saved with only name and timezone or a partial schedule; every supplied value is validated (ordering, future boundaries, complete-window overlap) and missing values show as unmet readiness. Invariant: effective opening (actual opening, else scheduled) < signup close ≤ event start < event end ≤ submission cutoff. Opening signup requires a future close and valid start/end plus the other readiness; a configured future automatic opening needs a future close and valid chronology. Scheduled mode = a configured future opening timestamp; manual mode records the actual opening.
- Unchanged timestamps keep their exact stored UTC instant (seconds, sub-seconds, repeated-hour history). Changed local times must be valid, unambiguous, on five-minute steps and in the future. Passed signup boundaries are history and cannot change or be cleared; draft time freezes at the actual draft start and is planning information only, never starting the draft. Published start/end cannot be cleared. Clearing signup close while signups are open is refused (a different valid future close is allowed) with a localized error on Signup closing.
- Before first actual Live, start and end may be repaired to future values even after they pass. While the draft is Running or Finalized before first Live, signup dates, draft time and capacity are locked; start and end stay editable. Every accepted change rechecks ordering, operational-window overlap and any linked Wise Old Man competition's exact UTC window; changing the end re-derives the cutoff and audits atomically.
- Routine pre-Live changes are audited automatically with no reason. Changing a future end while Live needs a reason and one consequence confirmation. Changes that affect participants after public reliance use one server-recomputed old → new confirmation showing only actual changes, derived cutoff effects and current warnings; draft-time-only and private warning-free saves happen immediately without a confirmation ladder. Server readiness is always rechecked. Legacy events whose automatic opening was disabled and is overdue keep that behaviour.

- Actual signup open/close, actual start/end, submission closure, finalization, archival and cancellation times are system history, never Schedule inputs. Hidden events expose only Super Admin restoration; discarded events expose no workspace.

### Board readiness during setup

- The board may be built and revised throughout signup and before the draft; it need not be complete when signup opens. Board validation and publication fix the competitive board (§8).

### How To and images

- `/HowTo` is source-controlled content with stable anonymous routes and no in-app editor: five anchor-linked steps (event discovery, signup, board progress, evidence submission, review tracking). There is no Rules page: How To is the public guide, linked from the Landing page ("How it works") and the shared public header navigation; upload instructions are not copied into tiles. It is not a lifecycle prerequisite.
- Application images use managed upload through one server upload route (local file, server validation, managed asset); the owning record stores a managed asset reference, never bytes or an arbitrary URL. Decorative images have their own authorization and retention. Old banner and team-image references authorize no controls. External source-image URLs exist only in the global catalogue, which fetches and caches them (§9).

## 6. Signup and roster

### Signup visibility

- An event stays private until signups are explicitly opened. Opening exposes the signup form and the unlisted signup table through exact links with minimal information (name, deadline, dates, instructions); they are not in general navigation or the public board overview. The signup table is distinct from rosters. Public event discovery lists signup-open events and links to the signup page; the exact link and My events also reach the form or confirmation. A returning owner goes to their existing record; signed-out entry goes through Login with a validated local ReturnUrl. A private or unpublished signup slug fails closed for non-Admins.
- The public signup table shows Confirmed and Waiting list sections, numbered exact waiting positions, close time, capacity, Confirmed count and current admission placement. Each row's event-facing name is the built-in primary regular character, never the username or Discord. It shows regular Account answers with EHB, alt answers without EHB, the captain-volunteer answer and every active participant-facing custom answer (missing optional answers as **Not answered**).
- The table never shows username, Discord identity, payment, Admin notes, audit or security data, inactive legacy questions or answers, private answers or co-captain answers. There is no per-question visibility toggle, admin-only custom question or post-draft privacy change; the legacy visibility field is fixed true. Alt answers never appear on rosters, evidence, progress or leaderboards.
- At draft finalization the signup page stays available to enabled Admins; public, participant and captain requests redirect to the published rosters. Closed, draft or finalized phases return the phase-safe destination. Roster pages never expose signup answers.

### Signup form

- Discord identity comes from authentication, not an answer. Registrations grant no authority or ownership.
- Fixed fields: the required primary regular Account with required EHB snapshot; the required captain-volunteer Yes/No; and the standard optional "Co-captain (optional)" single-line text question (Admins may disable and re-enable it), placed right after the captain volunteer and shown only while volunteering is checked (its value is restored if re-checked before submit; the server stores an empty answer when not volunteering). The co-captain answer is never on public tables or non-owner non-Admin draft projections and assigns no role. Required system fields cannot be removed.
- Extra Account questions are always optional and are either **Regular account** (PLAYING: requires its own EHB when answered; drop-eligible) or **Alt account** (INFORMATIONAL: a support character played alongside a main; no EHB; can never be active, credited or in Wise Old Man standings). A Yes/No question records mere existence and creates no assignment. Zero alt fields is valid. Existing "Account" / "Alt account" wording suffices; no extra role badges. My accounts labels and notes are not signup answers.
- Custom question types: Text (one multiline type), Number, Yes/No, Single choice and Account. Comments and availability are not fixed fields.
- Answer order: the primary regular account, then extra regular accounts, then alt accounts, in configured order.
- Before draft start, questions may be added, edited, deleted and reordered while signup is open or closed. Before the first accepted response the answer shape may change. After it, an existing question's type, Account role, choice options, stable key and answer shape are fixed; new questions are optional; optional cannot become required; labels, help text and order stay editable. A required custom add after the first response succeeds as optional with an explicit `CompletedAsOptional` outcome and explanation.
- Changing a format means delete then create. Delete is the only removal (no Hide, no backfill): its confirmation shows the current impact; it permanently removes the question's answers everywhere and releases only that question's registrations atomically, never deleting history or My accounts links; restoration never reacquires from deleted questions. Status, order, system questions and audit are kept.
- Signup form definitions are versioned. Every definition change increments `SignupForm.version` and is audited; the first-response marker alone (null → `FirstResponseAt`) does not advance the version. Changes are checked against the marker and structure in the same Serializable event-locked transaction; there is no stale-baseline bypass. Replay of an add uses its request identity plus its creation audit; a missing or corrupt creation audit fails closed. Draft start freezes the form and closes signup.
- Forms may differ between participants. Private views treat custom answers as optional history ("Not answered" without failing); inactive legacy questions and answers are private history only.

### Opening readiness and signup code

- Opening (Open now or scheduled) requires a public description, positive capacity, valid event start/end, a closing instant no later than the start, an allowed pre-draft state, configured Discord sign-in, intact system questions (primary playing field with EHB), valid custom definitions and, only when protection is enabled, a usable signup code. Open now needs no scheduled value; scheduled opening needs a valid future instant. A failed scheduled attempt leaves signup closed and shows its blockers.
- Missing banner, board, teams, questions, draft time or code protection are neither blockers nor warnings; Wise Old Man availability never affects readiness. Public short/long text answers and reopening a populated signup are informative warnings inside the single confirmation, never a separate acknowledgement or blocker.
- Admins may close and reopen signups at any allowed pre-draft time; reopening a form with responses needs confirmation and is recorded automatically, with no reason.
- An optional signup code (shared through Discord) gates first signup and self-rejoin only, not edits, withdrawal or Admin additions. Rotating it affects future admissions only. The code is at most 100 characters (longer is rejected before any change) and stored as a hash; enabling with a blank keeps an existing hash; enabling without a stored hash requires input; disabling clears it.

### Characters, reservations and EHB

- The Account selector lists the participant's linked characters with a link to My accounts for missing ones; there is no free-text creation. On a fresh signup only the primary defaults to the preferred character; others start at None. Optional answers can be edited or cleared while open; the primary cannot be cleared. An assigned character stays editable if later unlinked. Admin-created or imported participants may have assignments without a linked account.
- A character assigned to another participant in the same event is not selectable, enforced transactionally (partial unique `(event_id, osrs_character_id)` over unreleased assignments); it is allowed in other events. Confirmed and waiting signups both reserve every named Account answer. Withdrawal before the draft releases the reservations while keeping historical assignment rows; a released character may be registered by another participant, and a later signup creates a new assignment row.
- Create and edit are atomic across participant, answers, assignments, saved-EHB updates, snapshots, first-response marker, form version and capacity/status. Any reservation conflict or capacity race commits nothing: the form marks the conflicting answer ("already assigned in this event"), names the conflicting account and keeps the other values; a failed edit leaves the last saved signup. A stale form definition reloads keeping answers.
- Each regular assignment stores its own signup EHB snapshot. The control is prefilled from the My accounts saved EHB; saving the signup updates that default and captures the event snapshot; later My accounts edits never rewrite the snapshot. Only the primary snapshot is the draft sorting value; secondary EHB is not summed.
- An explicit "Fetch from Wise Old Man" action in My accounts and in signup/edit EHB controls updates the owner-linked My accounts character, never a participant or assignment. A failed fetch changes nothing. Manual entry in My accounts is the fallback; there is no inline manual EHB editor in signup. No Wise Old Man request happens on render, selection, save or keystroke (§14).
- The primary account becomes the active account automatically at event start; there is no initial-active choice. Closing signup freezes the account set and roles. During Live the participant may swap any number of times among the frozen regular accounts; swaps take effect immediately at server time, are append-only, are serialized with submission attribution, stop at event end and are never future-dated.

### Capacity and waiting list

- Signup window configuration: opening, closing, timezone and maximum confirmed participants. Maximum confirmed players is at least 1 and at most 10,000, enforced in the domain and every service path (including add-one-place overrides) with a clear error.
- States: **Confirmed** (within capacity, draft-eligible), **Waiting list** (after capacity is reached) and **Withdrawn** (inactive before the draft, with actor history). The form stays open until the deadline when capacity is reached; the waiting list is always enabled while open and has no disable control.
- Admission: `CONFIRMED if confirmed_count < participant_cap else WAITING_LIST`, computed before commit; confirmed count counts only CONFIRMED participants regardless of team inclusion or manual-team membership. Waiting position orders active waiters by `signed_up_at`, then `signup_sequence`. There is no manual reordering.
- Capacity is editable before team-draft lock, never below the confirmed count. On a capacity increase or freed place, `open_places = cap − confirmed_count` and the first `open_places` eligible waiters are promoted atomically with the capacity write or release, audit and notifications. Withdrawal before draft lock releases all assignments in the same transaction as promotion.
- Selected-person exceptions promote only that person and never anyone else:
  - **Confirm** a selected waiter uses free capacity if any; when full, an explicit confirmation adds exactly one place and confirms only that waiter.
  - **Move to waiting list** works only when full and another eligible waiter exists: it ends the participant's current team membership and leadership with retained history (the confirmation says so), keeps their reservations, enqueues them last and promotes the next pre-existing waiter, never immediately re-promoting them. It is rejected while places remain or no eligible waiter exists.
  - **Restore** uses current capacity or the end of the queue with a new sequence, never displacing promoted participants and never forcing Waiting while a place is open; an explicitly confirmed full-event restore-and-add-one-place alternative applies to that participant only. Restore needs the participant's reservations to still be available.
  - These keep everyone else's relative queue order. An explicit capacity expansion is a distinct intent, never inferred, and a repeated or stale request cannot expand capacity twice.
- Promotion writes in-site notifications in the same transaction to the participant (link to their confirmation) and every enabled Admin (link to participant management, naming event, participant and trigger), with fresh IDs and no private answers, payment, notes, OAuth or account data. Discord contact is manual.

### Participant self-service

- Signup requires a signed-in account and creates one owned participant record. Editing is allowed only while signup is open; closing revokes editing server-side.
- The confirmation page after create or edit shows Confirmed or Waiting with exact position, regular accounts with EHB, alts separately, the captain choice, custom answers and edit/withdraw availability.
- Editing never changes signup time, sequence, status or waiting position. While open the participant cancels through a separate confirmed action (a withdrawal that may promote the earliest waiter); signing up again in the same open window reuses the participant identity, reacquires reservations and joins the queue end with a new timestamp and sequence.
- After signup closes and before the draft starts, the participant may withdraw through a separate confirmed action (releasing reservations and promoting a waiter) but cannot self-restore or edit. Self-withdrawal stops at draft start; there is no Live withdrawal or eligibility-grace workflow.
- Every compound action (promotion, cancel, withdraw, rejoin, restore) commits atomically.
- Failures: invalid setup stays in its form; temporary service failures offer retry; the participant resumes from the authoritative destination.

### Admin participant management

- An enabled Admin manages the pre-draft signup pool on an accessible event in Draft, Signup open or Signup closed while the team draft is unlocked. Each mutation rechecks actor, lifecycle, participant/account identity, configuration, capacity and versions. Participant administration must not weaken public self-signup validation or add an unprotected mutation; existing forms and routes stay compatible.
- Placement, reservations, payment, audit and required notifications commit atomically. The result returns participant ID, status, placement/capacity outcome and validation/conflict information, so the UI never parses success text. Unavailable characters or capacity are shown before mutation; conflicting corrections fail atomically.
- Coverage: selected confirmation, waiting moves, withdrawal, restoration, question-free Add from saved accounts (including the explicit full-capacity +1), primary selection and event-only account correction. There is no external or accountless creation or owner transfer; recovery keeps the same account.
- **Add** picks an existing active website account; the server resolves its saved account IDs and stored EHB, maps selections to the configured Playing slots, sets captain volunteering to No and leaves other answers absent. It makes no Wise Old Man request, needs no signup code or questionnaire, and refuses forged link ownership, client-supplied EHB, extra slots and duplicate event assignments. Missing saved EHB is never fabricated as zero.
- Before team-draft lock an Admin may change which current Playing account is primary; exactly one supplies the draft EHB, each account keeps its own EHB, and extra accounts are bounded by the configured slots. Event-account corrections change event assignments and EHB only, never saved links, names, preferred order or saved EHB, and are not limited to saved characters; shared global links are allowed, event-local conflicts are errors. Both preserve status, order, non-account answers, unrelated accounts, historical/live attribution and global character names, and the chosen primary and its EHB read consistently in Participants, the draft and projections.
- Admins withdraw with one confirmed "Withdraw participant" action for any reason (optional private note, no reason); before the draft it releases reservations and promotes a waiter. Withdraw and Restore lock once the draft starts and never remove roster membership; finalized roster removal happens only through Teams (§7). The captain answer stays boolean Yes/No; withdrawal always auto-promotes (no suppress option).
- Payment is a private binary Unpaid/Paid (default Unpaid) on the participant; there is no payment on the public form and no Unknown/Waived/Not required state. Payment and private Admin notes stay editable and audited in every retained visible state (including active draft, Live, Finalized, Archived and Cancelled); Hidden and Discarded events are inaccessible. This never makes answers, accounts, queue or roster editable. Signup time is recorded automatically.
- Admin withdrawal, restoration and registered-account correction notify the linked participant in the mutation; payment, note and ordinary answer corrections do not. All record structured history without a typed reason.

## 7. Teams and draft

### Teams

- Team names are event-unique; stable URL identifiers survive renames. Saving an existing team preserves its stored affiliation; stored affiliation and image values are retained, not deleted, but are not edited. Team display metadata is writable until the event-start lock.
- Roster assignment uses a searchable participant selector showing primary account, EHB and captain-volunteer status; payment, signup times and withdrawal stay on Participants.
- Direct roster setup allows a missing configured end before the actual start and refuses only a configured past end, naming start and end.

### Draft inclusion and modes

- `IncludedInDraft` alone decides draft inclusion, order, turns, sizes and picks; affiliation and formation labels are descriptive and grant nothing. The drafted-team count comes from the teams actually created; roster sizes come from the confirmed included participants and preassignments; no Admin-entered target size is stored. Board-editor team-size estimates are separate (§8).
- With two or more included teams: Setup → Running → Finalized, with derived balanced sizes and an active normal-account Captain on every drafted team at actual draft start and finalization.
- With zero or one included team: Admins assemble rosters manually and **Finalize roster** without website-draft blockers and without fabricated Running state or picks. Unplaced confirmed players are a non-blocking note, repeated in the manual-finalize confirmation.
- Both paths validate roster integrity and must satisfy board, Wise Old Man and start readiness.
- Capacity counts Confirmed participants regardless of team inclusion; manual-team membership never frees a signup place and uses no draft turns. Adding to a manual team requires a Confirmed participant; manual membership changes are refused while the draft is Running.
- Inclusion or active changes and team removal recheck event and draft state in a transaction serialized with Start and both Finalize paths. Structural changes are refused outside Setup; the drafted-team set, inclusion and formation types lock permanently at the first pick, even if all picks are undone. A missed scheduled start does not block pre-Live corrections while the configured end is future.
- There is no Pause/Resume, no finalized-draft Reopen, no captain-operated shared draft and no automated turns or timers: Admins record captains' picks given by voice or text. Historical paused or reopened drafts are readable only.

### Captains

- Draft start and finalization need at least one current `CAPTAIN` on every drafted team (`CO_CAPTAIN` alone is insufficient); several Captains are allowed. Co-captain is a distinct role. Captains and co-captains occupy normal roster places in the size calculation.
- Captain and co-captain changes are Admin-only, team-scoped role transitions. They stay available for current members through Live and Awaiting final review while uploads are open, take effect immediately, survive authentication changes, are recorded in history and notify the participant. Withdrawal revokes the role.
- Live start needs no Captain. Removing the last Captain during Live is allowed with a prominent warning (an unresolved support warning) that grants no fallback authority.
- Live and final-review role changes update the public Teams role labels without changing membership or Wise Old Man data; earlier publications remain history.

### Running the draft

- The pick pool contains included confirmed participants; waiting, withdrawn and excluded teams' members are outside it. Every confirmed participant stays visible during the draft; drafted players show their team instead of disappearing. The available pool is recomputed after each pick from current membership and inclusion; a picked participant returns only through Undo.
- Final sizes differ by at most one; captains count as places. Turn calculation skips any drafted team whose roster exceeds the smallest drafted roster until others catch up; the compact per-team current/final projection comes from the same calculation. Start, every pick and finalization revalidate that preassigned members still allow final rosters within one of each other and that no included confirmed participant can be stranded; impossible preassignments block.
- Preassignment into a drafted team is possible only before the first pick; otherwise a Running draft locks participant and roster edits.
- Start clears prior order positions when no pick was ever recorded or `requires_fresh_order` is set (set once any pick was ever recorded); a separate Scramble draws a new random order. `first_pick_recorded_at` is retained.
- Undo is a repeatable transactional stack over the latest active pick, with no count limit: it marks the pick undone (kept but inactive), ends its membership, returns the participant to the pool and recalculates the next turn from the remaining ledger. A non-latest pick cannot be undone while later active picks exist.
- A Running draft has one server-enforced controller, who alone can start, pick, undo, cancel an eligible attempt and finalize; others follow live read-only. A confirmed, audited takeover immediately removes the previous controller's authority. The active page renews the lease; control expires after five minutes without a successful renewal (not without a pick). The controller's identity is hidden on the streamed board ("Another admin") and named only in the takeover confirmation. Mutations are guarded by control, version, identity and membership: never two picks per turn, double assignment or a silent overwrite.
- A private Running draft may be cancelled only after explicit latest-pick undos leave zero active picks (never a bulk undo); cancelling returns to a usable Setup with the cancelled pick history retained.
- Errors for a missing Captain, impossible distribution or lost control name the fix.

### Finalization and publication

- Website-draft finalization requires every included confirmed participant to have one active drafted-team membership and every drafted team to meet the derived balanced distribution.
- Finalization (website draft or manual roster) transactionally publishes the rosters and, for a website draft, the effective active-pick order. The public draft projection shows only active picks by effective overall pick number with participant and team; undone attempts, recorded-by, timestamps and corrections stay in the Admin ledger. Finalizing stays on Teams and offers the way to the Board.
- Roster or pick publication never starts the event or publishes the board. Board publication is a separate, separately confirmed transaction (§8) whose failure cannot roll back the draft; a board that is incomplete or not yet published leaves rosters public and the board private.
- A finalized draft cannot reopen. Original and undone picks and past publications are retained.

### Corrections before first Live and the Live lock

- Before the first actual Live, Admins may **Add** or **Remove** finalized roster members through separate confirmed operations that republish the current rosters atomically, preserving earlier publications, original picks and attribution, and never withdrawing the public roster. Add selects an existing website account (reusing its event identity) and its required Playing account and assigns Participant, Captain or Co-captain; the questionnaire may be blank; no signup code, signup capacity, final team-size cap or automatic rebalancing applies. A failed Add never undoes a committed Remove. Stale requests fail. These corrections never fabricate picks or change the original order.
- The first actual Live permanently locks membership and registrations, also after an early end. There is no Live withdrawal, replacement, vacancy, departure, promotion follow-up or ownership transfer; historical replacement eligibility stays readable. Current members' roles stay editable as above. Account disablement removes access, not the roster place.

### Captain draft information

- The captains' draft-information view reuses the signup-table projection with role-aware columns including non-public answers, never paid status, Admin notes, security or audit data. Only drafted-team captains and co-captains see it, until finalization; afterwards non-Admins are redirected from the signup route to the published rosters.

### Team focus

- Team focus marks a tile, row or column, is shared by the team's captains and is visible to current team members only, never to opponents, the public or non-member Admins. A participating Super Admin sees their own team's focus; other teams' focus is visible only through an explicit, visibly identified, read-only inspection per team, not preloaded or persisted. The Super Admin never edits focus without a captain role. Focus affects no official state.

## 8. Board and tiles

### Shape and lines

- Each event has one rectangular board with 1–8 rows and 1–8 columns (commonly 5×5 or 6×6). Creation makes an empty 5×5 board; final dimensions are set in the board editor, which may change them before publication.
- Resizing never relocates tiles. A safe grow or shrink keeps tile IDs and coordinates and goes through the edit lease and version, audit and approval invalidation. If an occupied tile would fall outside, the resize is blocked and dimensions, layout, approval and public snapshot stay unchanged. A dimension change after tiles are placed previews the affected positions and tiles.
- Changing a published board's structure needs explicit confirmation, an audit reason and recalculation of every team's board and line state.
- Rows and columns count as lines; diagonals do not. A completed tile counts once but contributes to its row and its column; overlapping lines count separately (a 5×5 board has 10 possible lines).
- The board editor holds the players-per-team planning estimate used only for board EHB planning. It never creates teams, sets roster sizes or constrains the draft, is not set at creation or in draft setup, is never silently replaced by actual rosters and stays adjustable after roster finalization without changing competitive values. It is editable only before Live and read-only from Live, including during corrections (clean refusal).

### Tiles

- A tile belongs to one board and holds a name, a public description, an optional custom image (upload, replace, remove), a board position, one or more objectives, its EHB and its published state. The tile's effective EHB is its tie-break value; there is no separate tie-break field.
- Server and client limits: tile name ≤ 80 characters (checked for new or changed names only), contribution weight 1–10,000, correction reason ≤ 2,000 characters. The default tile name is the selected boss names joined, or "New tile".
- The only rearrangement is drag/move and swap. There is no tile duplication, copy from an earlier event, import or reusable template.
- Tiles store only objective wording and custom completion criteria; general screenshot and submission instructions live on the How To page, and approval has no per-tile evidence-instruction requirement.

### Objectives and counting

- The tile type is chosen once: catalogue drop or manual. A tile may hold several objectives that must all be met (for example 5 Barrows pieces + 5 Moons pieces); all objectives in a tile are the same kind, never mixed, enforced in the editor and in authoritative create, update and approval validation.
- A drop objective is built by choosing bosses or activities, choosing eligible drops, setting the target, choosing whether duplicates are allowed, and optionally a higher contribution weight per drop.
- **Duplicates allowed** on: repeated copies keep contributing. Off: each selected drop contributes once unless another cap is set. "Unique drop" means a unique-table item, not "no duplicates".
- Each eligible drop contributes 1 by default; the board may set a fixed higher contribution weight per drop (for example a Scythe counts 2 toward 6). Submitters cannot override weights. Progress is capped at the target and never carries to another tile; one obtained drop counts for one tile only. Maximum-contribution caps exist in data but have no editor control.
- Example component objective (Voidwaker): three bosses, eligible hilt, blade and gem, target 3, duplicates off.
- Manual objectives cover non-drop tasks (time, restriction, kill count, speed, collection log, combat achievements). They still need evidence and approval, hold their own completion description, criteria and target quantity, and each approved completion contributes 1.
- Adding a drop tile is rejected, naming the drop, if any eligible drop lacks a stored catalogue GP value (explicit 0 is fine, null is not; manual tiles are exempt). Approval and publication revalidate it. This checks stored values only; a provider outage never blocks.

### Descriptions

- A blank description is automatic: the field stays blank with a localized placeholder and the preview derives the text from the ordered objectives and selected drops. Drop copy always shows the target quantity, uses the item name when there is one distinct item, otherwise the source names joined as alternatives; separate objectives appear in stored order; manual objectives combine their authored descriptions.
- Nonblank text is an Admin override that objective edits do not change; clearing it returns to automatic. Existing nonblank descriptions stay manual.
- Approval freezes the rendered text and its mode in the snapshot; the public board reads the frozen copy, unchanged by later edits. On a correction publish, automatic descriptions of unchanged carried-over objectives keep the item names frozen in the published snapshot, so a catalogue rename never changes untouched tiles. Discarding a correction restores the snapshot's mode and text.

### Tile EHB and points

- EHB is used for estimation, line balancing, contribution statistics and the final tie-break, never as other team points. Catalogue drop tiles need automatic EHB derived from the catalogue and may use an optional total tile override, set, changed or reset against the calculated baseline with an explicit change-override intent on submit. Manual tiles need explicit EHB. An override cannot repair missing catalogue EHB; that is fixed in the catalogue (§9, §10).
- The public board shows a tile's expected EHB as "Points: N" (Danish "Point: N"), rounded to the nearest whole number with a minimum of 1, for display only.

### Editing

- Board editing uses optimistic concurrency against the full board aggregate version; a stale save is rejected with a reload instruction. The board opens view-only; explicit edit mode takes one renewable lease, others see who is editing, and the lease ends on release, confirmed takeover or navigating away. It renews only through board activity and expires after five inactive minutes.
- The overview and on-demand editors keep Draft estimates current, invalidating only affected values; approval recalculates authoritatively. There is no Admin roll-group editing and no application bulk or Wiki import (the domain and operator mechanics remain). Counting language stays plain.

### Approval and publication

- An unapproved (Draft) board stores stable catalogue references with live derived names, images, rates and EHB; relevant catalogue changes recalculate every affected unapproved board. The catalogue owns source facts; the board owns objective configuration and snapshots.
- Approval is possible only when every grid position holds a tile and every tile validates; empty positions, invalid objectives or missing automatic catalogue EHB block it with diagnostics. Any enabled Admin may approve, without confirmation or a second approver. Approval uses the aggregate version, transactionally revalidates and locks the referenced catalogue versions, and freezes an immutable snapshot of catalogue values, configuration, rates, EHB, artwork references and calculations; later catalogue changes do not alter it. A stale board or catalogue version rejects approval without residue and reloads.
- An approved, unpublished board may be unapproved; any private competitive edit atomically returns it to Draft, keeping the superseded approval record and resuming live derivation. Private pre-publication changes need no written reason.
- **Publish board** is a separately confirmed action, offered only while the board is grid-complete, approved and private and the draft (or manual roster) is finalized; the command rechecks all of this in its own transaction and a failure leaves the draft unchanged. Publication uses the snapshot without recalculating from the catalogue. Published boards are historically stable. Event start is blocked until an approved board is published. After publication the public board, team and tile routes are the destinations.
- Board preview never changes state and has no approve or publish path (§16 Board).
- Tile artwork: a tile with its own image shows it; otherwise boss artwork chosen by one shared rule — requirements in position order, per requirement one image per boss family (the family's priority boss, then name), distinct images, at most four (`src/Bingo.Application/Catalogue/BossArtworkSelection.cs`). The team board and the Admin Board preview use the same rule. Team-board tiles use their default artwork; the special Chambers of Xeric, Vorkath and Araxxor artwork is off (the images stay in `src/Bingo.Web/wwwroot/images/public-board/bosses/`). A tile with several boss images shows one at a time at full size and fades to the next (6 s each, 1.5 s fade, staggered per tile; first image static with reduced motion; paused while the tab is hidden), on the team board and in the Board preview (`wwwroot/js/boss-art-fade.js`). Bosses of one family (e.g. Chambers of Xeric and its Challenge Mode) share one image, so they do not fade.

### Corrections after publication

- A published board is corrected through a private correction that needs confirmation and a reason, produces a replacement snapshot and preserves history. Corrected publication is allowed only in Signup closed, Live and Awaiting final review (terminal, cancelled, hidden and discarded events reject it). During the correction everyone keeps using the active published snapshot, and newly arriving evidence is protected.
- Objectives keep a stable identity, evidence links and progress through title and description edits. Once any submitted evidence (pending, approved, rejected, reversed or withdrawn) references an objective, changing its requirements or scoring, or removing it or its tile, is blocked; objectives without evidence stay editable. Replacement approval and publication recheck these protections and fail without partial change. There is no historical evidence repair.
- An Admin may discard an open private correction after confirming that all unpublished edits are lost: it atomically restores the working board from the active published approval with the original objective and drop identities and closes the correction, leaving the published snapshot, rules, submissions, contributions and progress untouched. It is audited and fails without partial change on stale, invalid or missing data.
- Team display metadata follows its own pre-event edit lock.

## 9. Catalogue

### Records and permissions
- The catalogue holds bosses/activities and their source drops. An item is an internal identity (name, image, active flag, optional external ID, notes) created and edited only while adding or editing a source drop; several source drops may reuse it. There is no standalone item workflow.
- Every enabled Admin creates, edits, deactivates and reactivates activities and drops (including the source-image cache). Changes are optimistic-concurrency protected (a stale edit aborts) and audited automatically without a reason.
- Activity edits use the add form's validation rules and messages before any write or audit. Category membership and the drop-edit item-name length are validated.
- Lifecycle is deactivate/reactivate. Permanent delete is Super Admin only (`RequireSuperAdmin`), needs confirmation but no reason, and succeeds only when a transactional dependency check finds no source-drop, board (including draft-board reference), snapshot, asset/cache metadata, import-review or other historical reference. A blocked delete offers deactivation; listing the blocking references is not required.
- Deactivated activities and drops are not offered for new tiles. A draft board, or an unpublished correction objective, that uses one cannot be approved until changed; approved and published boards are unaffected.
- There is no in-app catalogue import preview/apply; bulk catalogue changes are operator tooling. The catalogue snapshot loader runs only in CI, Development and manual tests, never in a production deployment, and refuses the retired per-drop context.
- Rate/EHB data may come from external sources (e.g. Wise Old Man) only through stable APIs, never scraping. WOM account-EHB lookup and the cached Live competition sync never turn the catalogue into an auto-synced feed.

### Drop rates and roll groups
- A rate belongs to the source–item connection; choosing a tile drop selects its source and that source's rate.
- Admins enter the full rate text, including an `N x` roll count: "3/1024" is one roll, "3 x 1/1024" three rolls. Human-readable values such as "2 x 1/1,024" and manual notes are accepted; rates may be conditional or non-convertible. Every role may enter rate text when adding, editing or reactivating; there is no separate roll-count input.
- The rate is the final chance of the specific item in the player's own name at the activity's agreed team size; conditional raid-table (parent) chances are resolved before entry.
- Admins may add any drop, including low-value or "troll" drops; it need not be a unique-table drop.
- Every new drop (also on a new activity) gets roll group `default`. Existing groups (`barrows-equipment`, `purple table`, `doom-1-16-aggregate`, `fortis-full-run-unique`) are preserved. The roll group is the only editable advanced mechanic: a calculation input editable only by a database-checked Super Admin with server checks, audit and concurrency, read-only for Admins. There is no separate roll-group management.
- Retired per-drop context ("Only after", `probability_scope`, `conditional_on_parent`, `parent_probability`, `assumed_participants`) is refused, never rewritten, on every write path for every role. The columns, records and snapshots stay as history.

### Team size
- Each activity has one agreed Team size: whole number ≥ 1, default 1, editable by every Admin next to Kills per hour in Settings and Add activity. It describes the strategy behind the rate and EHB and is informational only: it never multiplies probability or EHB and never changes snapshots.

### Shared items
- Add drop adopts an existing shared item only by explicit choice. Adoption separates shared metadata (name, image) from source rates; the intended fields and shared-image scope are explicit. Creation retries cannot duplicate or crash. Saving one form never silently saves or discards another. Item provenance, price invalidation and dependency-safe deletion are preserved.
- Renaming a shared item or changing its image (including Add-drop adoption with a different normalized image) shows one structured confirmation naming every affected activity; there is no per-activity split.

### Item values
- Every new item needs a fetched or manual value; 0 is valid and distinct from missing. API-backed prices refresh; manual values stay manual and an API refresh never replaces them.
- Untradeables, including components such as vestiges and the Araxyte fang, default to 0; the value of an assembled tradeable item is never substituted automatically. An Admin may set a manual catalogue value.
- Catalogue value changes affect future snapshots only. Event price snapshots are frozen; there is no event-price editor or event-price correction.
- Price guard: the operator-checked initial population is the trusted baseline (without a trusted value, initial population still needs operator checking). Catalogue API refresh and event-start price selection reject a candidate strictly below 50 % or strictly above 200 % of a positive trusted value (exact boundaries accepted) and flag any zero↔positive change, keeping the trusted value. The rejected candidate and time are persisted and reported through catalogue feedback, separate from mapping validity. A rejected start candidate uses the trusted catalogue value with rejection provenance. Manual and untradeable values take precedence.

### API mapping
- Activity and drop editors have a collapsed API section: the activity's WOM metric, and the item's OSRS Wiki item ID for prices. Suggest proposes an exact-name mapping; Validate and save shows the matched name/icon; Save without validating stays available (e.g. during an outage, which never blocks saving).
- Statuses not configured, verified, unsupported and temporarily unavailable stay distinct, with explanatory messages. A verified WOM mapping proves metric support only. Shared metadata changes are version-checked and audited.

## 10. EHB and board balancing
- The board editor shows EHB per tile, total, per row and column, lowest/highest line, absolute and percentage spread, and easy/hard line warnings; the ±25 % line markers are advisory only. There is no per-team workload panel or per-team EHB breakdown.
- Preview board shows the real responsive public treatment without admin controls and has no approve or publish action.
- Admins position tiles deliberately to balance lines; there is no random placement.
- Catalogue/drop tiles must produce a valid automatic EHB to pass validation (repair the data; no bypass). Only custom/manual objectives take a manually entered EHB.
- A valid calculated drop tile may have an optional manual total tile EHB override. The calculated baseline is kept and the override can be reset to it. The effective value feeds board/line estimates, proportional player statistics and ranking; it is event-local, never changes catalogue probabilities, KC or Luck, and cannot bypass missing or ambiguous calculation inputs. Submitted evidence locks the affected scoring.
- Unapproved boards derive names, images, rates and EHB from the current catalogue; a relevant catalogue change invalidates and recalculates their tile, row, column, total and per-player estimates and reloads the board projection. Board approval is the snapshot boundary: approved snapshots never change.

## 11. Evidence, submission and review

### Submission model
- A submission is one kill/reward, one drop, one screenshot, one tile requirement and one credited participant (self, or a teammate chosen by a captain), credited to that participant's active playing account at the server submission time, for the team derived from current membership.
- Validation at submit: the actor's role allows it; tile and drop are valid for the event; the credited participant is the submitter or a captain's current teammate; the server-derived character is the participant's active Playing assignment (never informational); exactly one image; server time ≤ active upload cutoff; the contribution fits remaining progress; duplicate and per-drop caps hold; one approved submission is never allocated to several tiles.
- A failed image upload creates no submission.

### Attribution and active account
- Participants submit only for themselves; current Captains/Co-captains submit for their own team by selecting a current teammate (never an account). A global Admin role adds no submission authority; Admin/Super Admin keep any genuine event role.
- Participants see no credited-player or account selector. The server resolves the credited participant's active drop-eligible account at `submitted_at` and snapshots it (`credited_osrs_character_id`); inactive and informational accounts can never be credited. Players submit before swapping away.
- Each participant has exactly one active playing account. The primary account is the planned starting account and becomes active at start. Only the participant switches, only while Live, unlimited times; a switch is effective immediately at server time (no future-effective switches), serialized with attribution capture, stored as an immutable transition with effective and recorded UTC times, and protected against double swaps. Event end closes swaps. Captain/Admin switching on behalf is retired. Timestamp precision, ordering and history are preserved. WOM aggregation over all Playing accounts is unchanged.
- Original submission time and credited account are immutable; pending edits keep both. Only a reasoned Admin correction changes the credited account before a decision.

### Upload window and drop time
- After event end there is a fixed 30-minute upload grace (cutoff derivation: §4). Only drops obtained inside the official event window are valid, even when uploaded during grace.
- The app checks only the immutable server submission time against the active cutoff (or an approved reopening cutoff). The screenshot (clan-event plugin UTC timestamp) is the drop-time evidence that Admins compare visually with event end and active-account history. There is no typed drop time, OCR, second timestamp or "upload within N hours" rule.
- Screenshot game time decides activity eligibility; upload time decides cutoff and ordering. Without a trusted screenshot time, review shows the paused intervals with guidance to verify, never an ineligibility verdict based on submission time.
- Between event end and cutoff, participants and captains may keep creating and editing submissions for in-window drops (captains for their team members). The cutoff closes all participant/captain mutations; history stays readable and Admin review continues.
- After cutoff: post-end drops are invalid, no new drops can be submitted, pre-cutoff evidence stays reviewable, public boards stay visible marked awaiting final review, and Admins finalize after resolving submissions.
- An Admin may reopen submissions after end/cutoff with an optional new cutoff, a reason and audit. Reopening extends only the upload window, never the valid drop window (unless the official end is changed separately), and grants no emergency authority. Cutoff processing records closure; captain roles stay historical and mutations fail through server-side state/window checks.

### Screenshot content and verification code
- A normal screenshot shows the player name, the game drop message, the timestamp overlay when the event rules require it, and the event verification code when enabled.
- The verification code is an event setting (enabled/disabled), custom or generated. A replacement takes effect immediately or at a scheduled time. It is editable in Draft, Signup open/closed, Live and Awaiting final review only while uploads are accepted, and read-only after upload closure and in terminal, cancelled, hidden or discarded states. Each submission snapshots the code interval active at its server submission time. Checking is visual only (no OCR); an Admin may approve a mismatch as an explicit exception.

### States and later attempts
- States: Pending (editable/withdrawable by the eligible credited owner and team captains), Approved (applied to progress), Rejected (with Admin reason), Withdrawn (removed before approval), Reversed (approved, then removed by an Admin). "Replaced" is a presentation derived from a retained historical resubmission link, not a stored status.
- Duplicate or unusable screenshots are Rejected with a reason; there is no request-changes or duplicate state.
- Rejected and Reversed submissions are immutable and never directly re-approved. A later attempt is an ordinary new Pending submission, allowed only while the upload window is open, with its own image, server time, attribution, validation, review and contribution; it has no predecessor link or one-child rule, and retries cannot create linked children. Historical predecessor links and the `Resubmit` review-action value are display-only.

### Editing, withdrawal and screenshot replacement
- While Pending and before cutoff, the credited owner may edit, replace the active screenshot or withdraw; Captains/Co-captains may do the same for their team's eligible submissions. Approved, withdrawn, replaced and reversed rows, and teammates' rows for participants, are read-only.
- A replaced screenshot is deactivated and retained as history; read-only states cannot replace. Admins never upload or replace another user's image.
- After the draft has started, self-withdrawal from the event directs the participant to an Admin.

### Admin review
- Admin decisions are Approve and Reject only. Approve is one click (as is approving a private board); Reject needs a reason (the inline reason submit is its confirmation); Reverse needs a confirmation dialog and a reason.
- Reject and Reverse require an explicit server `confirmed` flag; without it nothing is written and the response is "Confirm this decision before it is saved. Nothing was changed."
- Approve, Reject, Reverse and Edit are refused before any write when the expected version is missing or zero: "This decision wasn't saved because the page didn't say which version you reviewed. Reload the submission and try again." A stale decision is rejected. Participant Withdraw/Correct are unaffected.
- Review shows the immutable Submission time, the minutes after event end and the Latest clan event time (event end in UTC) to compare with the screenshot's `DD/MM/YYYY HH:mm UTC` stamp. When the credited participant swapped accounts, review shows the latest relevant transition in UTC; the full transition list is Admin/audit detail.
- Pending metadata correction (Admin, with a required reason): may change tile/requirement, qualifying drop (boss/activity derived) or credited account. The account pool is current or released Playing assignments in the event of current members of the submission's team, or of former members whose membership covered the upload time (joined at/before, not ended before) and whose latest assignment is Playing; Informational accounts are never selectable. The credited participant is derived from the account, never edited separately. The submission never moves to another team; saved accounts and rosters are not rewritten.
- A correction locks and version-checks, revalidates the whole submission, and stores original and new values. Server time, calculated contribution and evidence image are never changed and no time is invented; a changed target takes the destination's frozen weight. A correction that changes none of tile, requirement, drop and credited character is refused before any ReviewAction or audit: "Change at least one detail, or cancel." Approved submissions need reversal first; correcting and approving are two actions.
- There is no post-cutoff correction access for participants or captains. ReviewAction, notification and audit are written atomically.

### Approval order
- Approving a later upload is blocked only when it would reduce credit available to an earlier Pending upload for the same team and objective (claimed weights, remaining room, per-drop limits, duplicates), evaluated in `SubmittedAt` order with a deterministic tie-break inside the serialized approval. The earliest affected submission's ID and time are returned and linked; resolving it removes the reservation. When there is room for all, out-of-order approval is allowed. Approved history never changes.

### Reversal and integrity
- Approved evidence is authoritative for progress. Approved screenshot, credited participant/account, submission time, snapshot weight and review history are never silently rewritten.
- Reversal deducts the exact contribution by recalculation; recalculates tile, line, board, leaderboard and placement; reallocates freed capacity to later approved evidence up to its original eligible claim; records Admin, time and reason; preserves the submission, its original approval and history; and writes the main immutable audit entry in the same transaction.

### Visibility and notifications
- Approved evidence metadata, credited player and screenshot are public from the tile. Other teams never see pending progress. There is no hidden-but-approved state and no privacy hiding: submitters redact before upload; removal from public view is a reasoned reversal, and the team may resubmit as a new submission while the window allows.
- Current team members see the team's retained history, feedback and private evidence, including rows of departed members. Former members, anonymous users and other teams fail closed, except that an archived former credited owner may read only their own Rejected/Withdrawn detail and asset through account history.
- A rejection notifies the credited participant and the current authorized Captain/Co-captains only (not the whole roster). New vacancy/replacement notifications are retired (history kept). Pending reviews and scheduled failures remain operational actions.

### Team submission workspace
- Before and during the draft, drafted-team captains see the confirmed signup projection without payment, notes, security data or audit.
- During Live, `/Submissions` shows, for Captains only, the team focus and then team status totals, followed for every current member (including departed credited members) by the complete retained team ledger in all states with status/player/tile filters linking to detail. Teammates see the team focus read-only on the board. A Captain submits for a teammate only through the board drawer.
- Team focus is team-private, concurrency-protected, non-competitive, hidden from opponents and the public, and read-only after event end.
- Every detail request enforces team, owner, role, state, cutoff and privacy. Captains edit eligible team submissions; participants only their own. Captains never design boards, review evidence, manage rosters or grant authority. There are no Admin review controls in the workspace.
- A stale focus or submission mutation reloads team state. A removed role loses authority immediately. Unavailable or legacy records fail closed without leaking. Realtime updates never interrupt an active submission. Emergency credentials grant no authority.

## 12. Progress, ranking, leaderboards, Stats and Luck

### Placement order
- Each event's persisted ranking-rule boundary selects its order; only new events created through the ordinary creation service use the current order. Existing events and their official snapshots are never reranked.
- Current order: (1) full-board completion, earliest immutable submission time of the final qualifying submission first; (2) most completed rows and columns; (3) most completed tiles; (4) highest credited EHB = sum of the expected EHB of completed tiles only (incomplete progress adds nothing; public EHB/Drop EHB displays keep proportional credit; Final review and the official snapshot show and store this value); (5) earliest current-score completion time.
- Retained order for existing events (including those without official results): full-board status; full-board time; completed rows and columns; completed tiles; current-score completion time; credited EHB, highest first.
- Current-score completion time is the latest immutable completion time among currently complete tiles (null if none). Reversals use the surviving valid completions, never the reversal or review clock.
- Exact equality across all inputs gives a shared placement; there is no discretionary, name or ID tie-break and no manual timestamp correction.

### Winner
- The first team to complete the whole board wins and is marked as finisher and provisional winner pending reviews and finalization. The event stays open until the official end and Admin finalization so others can compete for remaining places.
- Full-board completion uses the immutable server submission time of the completing evidence, not review time, and is not editable.

### Player contribution
- Every submission credits the player who received the drop. The player leaderboard may show estimated EHB contribution, approved qualifying drops, approved submissions, tile-finishing drops and team.
- Each approved item submission is one actual item: scoring weight affects board progress only, never item count or GP; value is that of the exact dropped variant.

### Stats history and milestones
- Stats history uses submission time, never approval time, and counts only approved, non-reversed evidence. Out-of-order approvals recalculate historical totals, completion times and milestones in submission order; reversals recalculate from the remaining valid evidence.
- Official completion comes from the existing snapshots; there is no alternative official placement. Public Stats require a published board, a public event state and supported actual-evidence history. The imported Sommerbingo event is excluded from Stats.
- Event milestones are eleven event-wide stops: start, first approved submission, all teams submitted, first tile, all teams completed a tile, halfway, first row, all teams completed a row, first board, all teams completed a board, end. Team scope omits the four "all teams" stops and labels its board stop "Board completed". Start comes first; reached stops follow by occurrence; ties and unreached stops keep default order. Unreached stops say "Upcoming", after the event "Not reached" with a muted ×. Timestamps are never fabricated.
- Stats has no demo controls; its in-section tabs, searches, filters and help remain. Adjust artwork is the only bottom control: Super Admin only, persisted server-side, with per-item drag, zoom, rotation, Save/Cancel/Reset and desktop/mobile previews; original images and default fitting are preserved.

### Luck and KC calculation
- Both Luck modes project one saved calculation snapshot. A successful normal WOM fetch recalculates it from the returned activity and current approved evidence inside the sync transaction. Approval and reversal keep the compatible prior result with its original calculation/fetch times; they do not recalculate, clear or routinely warn. Ordinary age or evidence changes never invalidate a compatible saved result. Reads never fetch, rescore or publish.
- Final review may make one provider-limited refresh before publishing; a skip or failure keeps the prior result and never blocks publication. Changed state is revalidated before publish.

### Tile KC and Luck
- A published team board's selected tile shows a KC/Luck section like the EHB/Drop EHB sidebar: summary Team total, tile Luck and KC; expandable Contributors with Luck, name and KC. Each relevant boss/mode gets its own labelled KC, never combined. Only drops received for this tile count. The nested TeamBoard route and the enhanced Sidebar response share one presentation; the standalone Tile scaffold is not a registered route.
- Tile KC aggregates each participant's Playing accounts, counting each character/metric once regardless of repeated placement; informational alts are excluded. Contributors list only rows with known KC > 0 for the displayed boss/mode; zero/unknown rows, empty groups and an empty disclosure are hidden, without affecting team totals or Luck. Missing/estimated/incomplete/stale semantics are kept.
- The tile query uses the approved publication, frozen rates, cached WOM activity and Playing-account attribution, with no provider call. Failed, partial or stale fetches keep the last compatible KC and contributors with their timestamp until the next accepted fetch (including legacy results without tile breakdowns); age alone never clears activity, tile completion does not stop the interval, and a reversal does not invalidate before the next fetch.
- Tile Luck shows only when activity and evidence revision are coherent; otherwise KC stays visible and Luck is explained as unavailable. No-drop objectives show an empty state, never invented numbers. Visibility, privacy and controls are the same on enhanced and direct routes.

### Leaderboards
- Public Leaderboards has a metric selector. The default "EHB & Drop EHB" keeps the EHB, Drop EHB and Players views. With WOM linked, the distinct relevant boss/mode metrics are offered alphabetically, each with Teams and Players views of full-event gained KC from cached activity and Playing-account attribution. Selecting a metric never fetches provider data.
- Boss Teams columns: Rank, Team, Players, Total gained, Avg. gained, MVP. Players counts participants with known positive gain and the average divides by that count; expansions and the Players view show only contributors. Zero teams stay visible without MVP or expansion. A participant's accounts combine; informational accounts are excluded. Players columns: Rank, Player, Team, Gained, Start, End, Drops.
- Missing data never becomes zero. Leaderboard views show no freshness/update/health text or timestamps. KC is never duplicated through repeated tiles or added into heterogeneous totals.
- Drops is the participant's approved event drop count for that boss across tiles, a coral clickable number opening the Drops view with the boss name searched and the participant's team selected (the whole team's matching drops).
- MVP (boss KC and EHB/Drop EHB team tables) shows the name and individual signed contribution. Tied top contributions show "Multiple MVPs" with the common value, never the sum; no contribution means no MVP. Signed formatting has no units; positive gains and MVP values use gain-green with "+"; raw Start/End stay raw. EHB/Drop EHB calculations and visibility are unchanged. The heading is "Rank" in English and Danish.
- Switching metric updates in place without full navigation or scroll to top, fetching only the selected view through the existing owners (no preloading every boss, no WOM fetch). Controls, standings metric, URL, Back/Forward, sorting and expansion stay coherent; an older response never replaces a newer selection; a failure keeps the last usable view with recovery; direct navigation and reload work.

## 13. Final review and official results
- Finalization is an explicit Admin action, reachable from Manage after end and cutoff.
- Publishing requires a closed upload window, zero Pending submissions and valid calculated placements, plus lifecycle and version checks. Every blocker links to its correction. There is no checkbox, blocker override, completion-time edit, inspection acknowledgment, manual tie resolution or placement-correction control.
- Publish needs one confirmation and no reason. One transaction stores an immutable official result version and enters Archived (previous-event history, same public URLs); there is no separate Archive action. Version numbering and old versions are preserved; legacy Finalized stays readable. The optional final WOM refresh may skip or fail without blocking.
- Stale readiness, a stale competitive input or version, concurrent finalization or an unresolved blocker fails before any official mutation; a duplicate publish does nothing more. Historical resolutions are read-only and never satisfy current gates.
- Reopen (exceptional) needs strong confirmation and a reason. It supersedes the current placement snapshot, returns results to provisional and Final review without reopening uploads, keeps every earlier version and finalization history, and creates a new correction cycle and version. It is blocked while another production event is current. A missing, zero or stale expected version refuses Reopen with no transition or audit.
- Publish and Reopen return structured state and keep legacy redirect compatibility.
- After an event closes, Captain roles are historical and cannot mutate it; assigning a Captain never generates a password; emergency credentials cannot regain authority.

## 14. Wise Old Man

### Role and window
- WOM is read-only and supplementary: cached activity never changes snapshots, credit, readiness or finalization, and provider failure never blocks a valid lifecycle transition, early end or Resume.
- Website dates own the schedule; WOM dates are never imported and there is no sync toggle. The competition's start and end must equal the website's configured start and end as UTC instants exactly (no tolerance) at linking, replacement and every window check including Final review. Timezone display differences of the same instant match. Website dates are never silently changed; provider/persistence precision is reconciled without rounding away real differences. Existing links are never silently disconnected or snapshots rewritten. Actual start/end instants still drive cutoff, drop eligibility and review and never substitute for this check.

### Connections and credentials
- Linking an existing competition (Admin, via event setup) waits for any Sending/Unknown website Create so a late completion cannot overwrite the new link.
- Website-created connections store the link and protected management credential automatically and may sync and, before first Live, be deleted remotely. ID-only external links are provider-read-only (no updates). External links with a protected supplied code may sync but are never deleted remotely. Adopting a credential never changes provenance. Credentials are validated without mutation where supported, else on the first operation with an honest unverified status; secrets are never redisplayed.
- An external competition may be disconnected locally before first Live and replaced by a matching competition before or during Live, with or without stored credentials. Both are blocked while a provider operation is active or unresolved; the old local connection is retired so no stale write targets it. The external competition is never deleted and its code never carried to the replacement. A Live replacement cannot clear or resync the schedule. Final review and terminal-state connection configuration is read-only.
- Eligibility, reasons, next permitted time, credential and operation outcomes are structured. The external-deletion prohibition, cooldown, scheduled slot, lease, operation and history guards are never relaxed; conflicts are never overwritten or re-sent. Cooldown wording stays generic.
- The Admin WOM page owns WOM operations; the event Overview only summarizes and links to it.

### Competition creation and roster
- Create competition (WOM page, finalized pre-Live event) submits immediately; there is no chosen future creation time, and start/end come from the Schedule. Pending, retry and reconciliation run in the background; scheduled fetches and future event dates never schedule creation.
- The payload is one whole-team roster from every active membership and unreleased Playing assignment of confirmed participants, including non-draft teams. Local validation rejects malformed or duplicate names, missing assignments, an incompatible schedule and any team with no eligible Playing account ("Cannot create WOM competition with empty teams.", naming the teams) before any request; there are no existence lookups. New or changed names are at most 50 characters (event) and 30 (team) with provider-compatible normalization; overlength historical names are reported for correction, never truncated.
- The provider's response is shown. Provider errors keep the roster as retryable, corrective or unknown; no automatic second Create after a timeout. Claims coalesce and stale responses are fenced. Link and receipt are saved together.
- The managed desired state covers the UTC schedule, active finalized draft publication, active memberships/teams, participant status, unreleased Playing assignments, provider-normalized names and identity mapping. Permitted pre-Live corrections enqueue one coalesced update through the provider limiter.
- After the first actual Live, WOM participants are permanently locked: no new roster or delete write or retry (an already dispatched pre-Live request may finish and reconcile). A Live end correction sends dates only. Local history is never rewritten to match WOM.

### Remote deletion
- Deleting the remote competition is a separate confirmed action, only before the first actual Live, needing current authority, a fresh link/version, protected credentials and explicit target confirmation. It removes only the remote competition, never local history. Cancel, reopen, unlink and lifecycle changes never imply deletion.

### Fetching
- Fetches run in fixed hourly UTC slots anchored to the actual Live start (first slot at start + 1 h), during Live and Awaiting final review; start triggers no immediate fetch. Delayed, manual, urgent or retry requests never move the anchor; after downtime one current due refresh runs, no burst. No anchor means unscheduled. Fetching stops in terminal states, keeping the cache.
- Fetch now is a normal action without typed confirmation, subject to server eligibility, successful-fetch cooldown, slot/retry timing, lease and in-flight protection, the unmatched-end guard and sync guards. There is no manual update-all. The Development make-due action is Live-only.
- Before publication one normal refresh may run under the hourly/cooldown/lease rules, with HTTP outside the finalization transaction and concurrency revalidated afterwards; there is no refresh after archive.
- Calls are never per viewer, player or team. Readback never performs a fetch, and an old success never proves a new operation.

### Activity and public display
- Every Playing assignment contributes its cached delta; a participant's regular accounts sum, informational alts are excluded. Team average is participant-based; tied top contributors share MVP.
- Missing accounts get no zero or carried value; zero matches show no rankings. If the newest result misses an expected account, public rankings are withheld even when an older complete cache exists. A compatible older cache stays visible without freshness banners. Public projections show only matched totals, provisional/partial state and coverage counts; missing names are Admin-only.
- Rate-limited, unavailable, malformed, not-found and partial responses give accurate retry/incomplete/manual feedback and never clear entered EHB or the My Accounts value. There are no WOM notifications.
- WOM end values use each player's last snapshot inside the window (players are expected to log out right before the end); there is no compensation logic.

### End updates
- Early end and Resume always succeed locally; the WOM end update follows. The worker attempts immediately, then retries after 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 minutes and every 30 minutes, honouring provider retry times (never a fast loop); worker passes do not reset the due time. A past end is accepted by WOM if not before start.
- Temporary: 5xx, timeouts, network failures, 408, 429. Permanent: HTTP 400 `COMPETITION_START_DATE_AFTER_END_DATE` and other validation rejections, missing/invalid code, 401/403/404. For an end-only unknown write, reading the unchanged old window proves it was not applied and permits a spaced resend; reading the target window confirms success. Other unknown operations keep their protections.
- Status: NotRequired, Pending, Succeeded, Rejected, CouldNotUpdate, with target end and sanitized failure. The WOM page shows Pending/Rejected/could-not-update with the target end and paused-fetch wording; Overview shows Needs attention in Final review for Pending/Rejected, linking to WOM; the Final review publish confirmation warns that the last fetch becomes official, and the note is kept per result version.
- While the end is unmatched, no fetch runs after the actual end; fetches resume after a successful update.
- The first official publication stops pending end updates and reconciliation for good, even after reopen; reopened/republished results keep the same WOM basis. If still unmatched at publication (outage, missing/invalid code, permanent rejection) the event is marked "WOM end could not be updated" (CouldNotUpdate), the final fetch is skipped and the last fetch before the actual end is the official WOM data; Luck freshness shows that fetch's age, with no invented values or zeros. ID-only links always take this fallback after an early end. Publication is never blocked.

### EHB lookup and validation
- WOM EHB lookup is an explicit per-account control after a name is entered, in My accounts and signup/edit; never on render, typing, selection, save, public view or lifecycle transition, and never a prerequisite for linking. It may report not found.
- A successful signup/edit lookup fills the EHB field and immediately updates the owner's linked My Accounts character without creating a participant or assignment; manual entry stays available; the submitted signup stores the manual or fetched snapshot. Lookups use the server cache, throttling and `Retry-After`/backoff; failure keeps the manual value.
- WOM metric validation reads boss keys from `/v2/efficiency/rates?type=main&metric=ehb` through the shared limiter and never imports rates. The default WOM User-Agent names DKLegacy with the operator's Discord contact; an override must keep a contact-bearing structured User-Agent.

## 15. Drop announcements and updates

### Eligibility and delivery
- Public Board/TeamBoard use one live announcement queue (no generic progress-refresh notice). Each approval creates exactly one update (no separate completion announcement); pending, rejected and reversed evidence never appears.
- Only an enabled account of a Confirmed participant with current membership in an active team of a non-hidden Live or Awaiting final review event receives announcements and NEW state; Admin privilege alone does not qualify. Enforced server-side.
- Delivery is event-wide (every team's approvals). Tracking starts at the later of the event's persisted tracking start and the membership join; earlier history is excluded, offline approvals are kept, and there is no historical backfill. Deployment never marks future approvals as seen.
- There is at most one active scheduled event; no multi-event queues or event selection.
- Finalization advances the tracking generation: it clears the banner queue, every Drops NEW mark and the DROPS nav badge for every account (including offline) and stops announcements, while feed entries and evidence remain. Connected and returning clients reconcile; stale reads or reopening never resurrect cleared updates.
- A reversed approval leaves banner and NEW eligibility; a later approved attempt is a new update. Announcements change no progress, evidence or approval semantics.

### Banner
- The banner shows on every non-Admin page, including landing and account pages. Admin pages hide it without acknowledging. Navigation keeps queue, selection, expanded/compact state and remaining countdown without replaying entry.
- Title and artwork: the achieved item for a drop; the frozen tile name/artwork for a non-drop objective; never an evidence screenshot. Missing artwork tries item then tile artwork, else text-only without an empty frame or placeholder icon, banner dimensions unchanged.
- Kicker: `New tile progression: {progressAfter} / {target}` (EN/DA), or `Tile completed` without counter when this approval completes the team's tile; completion is green, progression coral. It describes this approval's effect.
- Compact label `N NEW UPDATES` (localized singular/plural) with Expand and dismiss. Manual previous/next updates artwork, title, player, team and action together, is disabled at the ends, and is omitted with the counter for a single entry.

### Expansion and cooldown
- The first eligible arrival opens expanded when allowed. New arrivals append without stealing selection, resetting the 10-second countdown (client-local) or extending the cooldown.
- Automatic expansion atomically claims a two-minute cooldown per account and event, stored server-side (survives reopen, refresh, tabs and devices); dismissal restarts it; concurrent claims yield one expansion.
- Automatic expansion needs an expired cooldown and an outstanding approval newer than the persisted last-auto-expansion boundary, stored atomically with the cooldown and advanced only over the claimed snapshot. Navigation, refresh and return use the same rule; there is no timer-driven expansion.
- During cooldown, arrivals update the existing banner (or show compact if dismissed); expiry alone changes nothing; active interaction, submission and result states are never interrupted; manual Expand is always available.
- Automatic expansion selects the newest approval of its claimed queue; manual expansion restores the last selection. Order is approval chronology with a stable tie-break. Acknowledged or reversed items never qualify.

### Acknowledgement and NEW state
- Banner and Drops NEW are two independent, durable per-account/event states. Acknowledged announcements never return, robust to connection loss, out-of-order or duplicate invalidations, stale tabs and retries, without losing later approvals.

| Action | Banner acknowledgement | Drops NEW acknowledgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dismiss | Displayed queue snapshot only | None |
| GO TO DROP with popup opened, or opening the popup in Drops | That approval | That approval |
| CLEAR ALL NEW | Approvals covered by the action | All current NEW in the event |
| Finalization | Everything, every account | Everything incl. badge, every account |

- Navigating to Drops, compacting, waiting, switching entries or a failed popup load acknowledges nothing; opening the popup is the view boundary; viewing one never clears others. GO TO DROP opens the approval's event Drops view and its evidence popup even outside the first 25 or the filter, then selects the next entry and compacts, or hides when empty.
- CLEAR ALL NEW applies across filters and pages up to a server snapshot (later approvals stay new). Success shows a localized confirmation toast; failure shows the error toast, never success. Acknowledgement mutations are authenticated, ownership-scoped, anti-forgery protected and idempotent.
- Each new entry has a NEW mark; Drops has CLEAR ALL NEW; a small coral NEW sits beside DROPS in the event nav while any update is new. The badge follows Drops NEW, not banner dismissal, and syncs across visits and devices. Personal-notification read state is not reused.

### Live updates
- Approvals update banner and badge live. An open Drops view inserts entries live, keeping scroll, filters and popup; inserted entries use real approval age and existing timestamps are kept; unloaded older entries are not promoted into the newest group. Filter-excluded arrivals still update banner and NEW. Reversal removes stale entries without disturbing popup or submission. No full reload, board redraw or interruption.

### Personal notifications
- A rejection creates one idempotent in-site notification with the reason for the credited participant and every current Captain/Co-captain (captains even when the participant is unlinked). Personal recipients open `/Submissions/{id}` with independent authorization; general submission navigation goes to `/Submissions`; Admin review notifications open `/Admin/Review/Details/{id}`.
- A waiting-list promotion creates a durable in-site notification (event, participant, trigger) for the linked participant and every enabled Admin; the confirmation page shows the current status. There are no automated Discord messages.

## 16. Admin pages

Each subsection says what an admin page shows, its controls, wording, states and URL behaviour. The feature rules a page exercises live in the sections it points to. Shared components, feedback patterns and layout are in UI.md.

### All admin pages

- Cancelled, Finalized and Archived events open every admin event page read-only; every change is refused by the server.
- Hidden or discarded events return 404 on every event page except the Super Admin's limited hidden view on Overview.
- Outcome vocabulary used below: *failed* means the server says nothing changed (entries kept, retry offered); *stale* means another admin changed the record first (nothing saved, current values shown, entries kept); *uncertain* means the response was lost (fields lock, the action becomes Check again, which only reads current state back and never repeats the request); *refused* shows the server's reason. A readback that matches the intended values says "Up to date" and never claims this request saved it.

### Dashboard

Route `/Admin`. One read-only page of community statistics for enabled Admins. It saves nothing, never asks a provider to sync and never changes lifecycle. Admin actions are in the inbox at `/notifications`, not here.

- **Population.** Totals, chart and history include non-hidden Live, Awaiting final review, Finalized and Archived events the admin can access; Cancelled and Discarded are excluded. Totals and breakdowns share one population and one request clock. Hidden events never leak through totals, history or links. The sidebar event selection is navigation only, never a filter on Dashboard totals.
- **Headline figures:** events held, unique website participants, total event participations, approved submissions; optional EHB gain with coverage.
- **People counting.** A person counts once per qualifying event participation regardless of several accounts or team moves. Unique and returning people are identified by website account only, never guessed from character names. Imported or unlinked records count as participations without invented identity. Disabled accounts keep their history and count; emergency credentials never count. Returning means seen in an earlier actual-start cohort; events with equal actual starts share a cohort.
- **Unique people** = sum of first-time people, except that a person first seen in several equal-start events is first-time in each bar but counted once in the headline (unique 1, bars 1 + 1); never sum equal-start bars to derive the unique figure. **Total participations** = sum of bar totals. Exclusions apply consistently.
- **Approved submissions** exclude reconstructed imported contributions. **EHB** is the stored competition period gain with truthful availability and coverage, never signup EHB. New-account figures use the latest actual event end, else the last 30 days; login figures use stored `LastLoginAt`.
- **Latest-event additions:** +new unique people, +participants, +approved submissions as actual values including zero; omitted when the latest event supplies the whole metric; definitions, coverage and provisional state go in hints; no percentages or trend lines. The latest contribution may come from an ongoing event.
- **Participation chart.** First-time/returning split with keyboard and touch details. Imported history has no account links, so tracking starts at the first platform event, marked "Tracking starts" with no returning split; imported bars stay unsplit; later bars split first-time/returning summing to that event's eligible linked population; unlinked records get explicit coverage handling. The accessible label shows unavailable measurements as unavailable, never zero; no invented bars.
- **History table**, six sortable columns: event/date, players, approved submissions, winner board, EHB, winner. Default newest first; the Event header sorts by date; Winner board sorts by completed/total ratio with missing or zero denominators protected; missing values sort last in both directions; event ID breaks ties. Sorting reorders rows in place from server order, keeps focus and scroll, replaces the URL; no fetch or skeleton. Sort survives Back and reload.
- **Recap** of the latest ended event by authoritative dates: name, date range, player and team totals, official winner, completed/total winner tiles; approved count and optional EHB secondary. All shared first places are shown in recap and history.
- **Provisional and official.** Live and final-review figures are labelled Provisional; the Live approved-submissions hint ends "Figures are provisional and may still change." In final review show provisional results, not a winner or winner board. Official winners come only from an active official result snapshot (shared first place preserved); the last official winner is shown only with its own event name; after results are reopened no old snapshot is presented as newly official and no provisional winner substitutes for it.
- **Current/upcoming card**: latest-start Live event (labelled Current, not next), else the earliest scheduled preparation event (including overdue, shown as overdue), else unscheduled setup by stable ID. Shows phase, the relevant next date, confirmed/capacity and a workspace link; hidden when none qualifies; unset capacity or dates shown as unknown, never fabricated.
- Community account snapshot.
- **Unknown is not zero.** Linked competition without compatible stored EHB: "—" with "No compatible stored EHB coverage is available." (not "unlinked"). Unknown platform approval: "—" with "Published board approval or a usable event interval is unavailable." in headline, recap and history. Unknown lifecycle interval for participation hints: "A usable actual lifecycle interval is unavailable. Unknown is not zero."
- Not shown: peak waiting list, fill speed, everyone ever waitlisted (not rebuildable from retained data).
- **Links.** History event names, chart bars, recap Open event and the current card open that event's Overview by stable ID; Go to Events opens the Events directory; no placeholder toasts.
- **States.** No events: one explanatory state with Go to Events, plus community figures and card if any. Imported only: no one-bar comparison, expanded recap, unknown unique/submission metrics explained. One tracked event: tracking-start meaning, no returning claim. Several: chart, history, recap. Loading, error and Try again; retry is a safe read and an outdated response never overwrites a newer one; lost authority fails closed. Read failures, cancellation, authorization failures and provider exceptions propagate as errors, never as zero results.

### Events

Route `/Admin/Events`. Directory of events with Create.

- **Populations.** Views All, Current/upcoming (Setup, Signups open, Signups closed, Live) and Past (Final review, Finished, Archived, Cancelled). Hidden is a separate Super Admin-only view (rechecked in the database) excluded from ordinary rows and counts; Discarded never appears. Search (event name only, not slug), phase filter and `attention=1` (attention count > 0) narrow the selected view. Results carry the population size before filtering, nullable capacity, retained participant availability and import provenance so empty and filtered states are honest. Server paging.
- **Default order:** Live first; preparation by scheduled start, then signup opening, then closing; unscheduled preparation last; then one newest-first past population using actual end, falling back to finalized, archive or scheduled end; Cancelled uses cancellation time; missing dates last; ties by event ID.
- **Columns.** Event: name linking to its Overview, Imported pill for imported history; no slug, Workspace button or row menu. Phase. Dates: range or "Not scheduled" plus one context line (Live "Ends in 4 days"; Signups open "Signups close 15 Jun"; Setup "Signups open 1 Oct" or "Starts in …"; Signups closed "Starts in …" or "Start was due …" after a failed start; Final review "Ended 10 days ago"; Cancelled "Didn't take place"), with a timezone suffix when not Europe/Copenhagen. Participants: while preparing confirmed / capacity with meter plus "N waiting", "Full" or "N spots left", "No capacity set" without capacity; Live "N players"; finished "N played"; Cancelled "N confirmed when it was cancelled"; missing import capacity never invented. Live and past participation reuses the Dashboard population rules (including departed people); missing intervals show unavailable; read failures propagate, never zero. Needs attention.
- **Needs attention** priority: unresolved scheduled start failure ("Start postponed", not "Start failed"), then signup opening failure ("Signup opening failed"), then pending review; "+N" counts further issue categories (the review queue counts once). The shared inbox still counts per submission plus each failure. Ordinary unfinished setup is never attention. If attention cannot be read, a partial "Needs attention unavailable" banner shows while rows stay usable. "N need attention" in the summary toggles `attention=1`, shown as a removable chip.
- **Sorting:** name, phase (lifecycle order), dates (unscheduled always last), participants, needs attention (failures first, then review count); first click uses the column's natural direction; "Default order" in the footer restores the default.
- Cancel, archive and discard live in the event workspace, not here; no directory drawer.
- **States:** loading skeleton; load failure with Try again (focused); "No events yet" with Create event; "No matching events" with Clear filters; empty Current, Past and Hidden views. Create stays available.
- **URL:** `view` (current/past/hidden), `phase` (state keys such as draft, signupopen, awaitingfinalreview), `search`, `attention=1`, `sort` (identity/state/dates/signups/attention), `direction` (asc/desc), `page`; only non-default values appear. Unknown values fall back to defaults; a phase outside the view is dropped; legacy `?filter=<state>` maps to phase and `?filter=hidden` to `view=hidden`; invalid parts (including Hidden for an ordinary Admin, who then sees All with an explanation) are dropped with a short notice. Opening an event pushes history; Back restores the directory from its URL with scroll position and focus on the row's link.
- **Create dialog** (rules: §5). Route-backed: `?create=1` on the current directory query; opening pushes, closing goes Back and keeps filters; a direct visit renders the directory underneath and closing replaces the entry with the plain directory. `/Admin/Events/Create` GET and non-dialog POST redirect to `/Admin/Events?create=1`; the dialog posts to it as JSON. Fields: event name (required, 50 characters, counter near the limit) and timezone (default Europe/Copenhagen; supported list Europe/Copenhagen, UTC). A non-blocking notice appears when an event with the same name exists (hidden events only for the Super Admin). Validation on submit keeps errors at the field, focuses it and keeps values.
- While creating: fields, Cancel, Escape and backdrop disabled; button "Creating…"; no duplicate submission. Failed: error banner, values kept, Create event available again. Uncertain: warning banner, fields locked so a retry cannot change what was sent, button becomes Check again (`?handler=CheckAgain&requestId=…`); if the event exists it opens its Overview with a toast, otherwise "It wasn't created" and Create is available again. A CheckAgain or same-key retry 404 says the event was not found and may have been removed; a fresh Create uses a new request key and keeps typed values. Leaving after an uncertain outcome offers Check again / Leave anyway and warns the event may already exist; ordinary dirty input offers Keep editing / Discard; never an automatic resend; beforeunload prompt while the form has changes.
- Success saves a private Draft and replaces the create URL with its Overview; Back returns to the directory with the new row highlighted once; neither Back nor reload can submit again.

### Overview

Route `/Admin/Events/Manage/{id}`. The event landing page: where the event stands, what needs attention and what to do next. Cross-event statistics stay on the Dashboard. Overview summarizes and links; Identity, Schedule, Signup setup, Participants, Teams / Draft, Board and Wise Old Man own editing, and Final review owns publication and reopening (lifecycle rules: §4).

- **Progression** Setup → Signups open → Signups closed → Live → Final review → Archived: done stages show actual moments, upcoming ones the scheduled moment or "Not scheduled". A postponed automatic start shows Live overdue ("Was due … · postponed"), never done. Cancelled ends at a "Cancelled" stage; legacy Finalized events show the last stage "Finished" with an archived-style results panel.
- **Needs attention** lists only actual failures and pending work, each once: "Automatic start postponed" in every pre-Live phase with the phase reason ("Open and close signups first.", "Close signups first." or the remaining start requirements, or "ready now"); failed automatic opening "Signups didn't open automatically: ‹reason›", styled like the postponed start, with Open signups now; submissions to review while Live; failed WOM sync while Live, linking to Wise Old Man and saying lifecycle actions aren't affected (not an Events directory attention item). In final review outstanding reviews are a publish requirement instead. There is no header blocker count; Needs attention and the checklists own readiness.
- **Current-stage panel**, one per phase with a short lead. The next transition shows its scheduled moment with the manual control (e.g. "Signups close automatically on 15 Jun" + Close signups now); with nothing scheduled it says so with a Schedule link. Only controls for the current phase appear, recomputed from current state so a phase change never leaves a stale control actionable.
- **Requirements** are neutral and tied to the next transition, each linking to its owning page; completed items may show checks; unfinished preparation is never an error or repeated count. Setup "Before you can open signups": description (Identity), capacity (Signup setup), event window and closing time when opening is automatic (Schedule), signup form (Signup setup). The Open/Reopen checklist lists every applicable server refusal with a repair link (Discord configuration, usable code, valid questions, public-event overlap, future close); a blocked Reopen lists rows under "Before you can reopen signups". Signups closed "Before you can start the event": draft finalized, board published, drop values, playing accounts, end still ahead. When another event is current (Live or Final review) the start checklist and postponed start say "Publish the results of ‹other event› first" linking to it (also in the Resume dialog); for a legacy Finished current event: "‹X› is still the current event. Contact the Super Admin to archive it." Final review "Before you can publish official results": three rows (uploads closed, every submission reviewed, placements calculated from the server's calculated-placements blocker) plus "N more on Final review" for other blockers. Later preparation is a quiet one-line note. A control whose requirements are unmet is aria-disabled with the checklist as its description.
- **Confirmations** list actual consequences. Open signups: makes the event public, when it closes (scheduled or proposed fallback), turns automatic opening off, later timezone changes need review; warns text answers are public when applicable. Close signups: places kept, the scheduled close is no longer needed, can reopen until the draft locks. Reopen signups: only before the draft locks, places and order kept, when it closes; no "reopening keeps existing signups" warning. Start event: one confirmation, no reason; explains early or postponed start, upload window, fixed drop values, first WOM refresh. End event: reason only before the scheduled end; states the ceiling-minute end; uploads stay open 30 minutes after the actual end; the event moves to final review and can be resumed.
- **Other actions** beneath the panel. Recovery (ordinary buttons, final review only): Resume event (reason and validated future replacement end), Reopen uploads (future time and reason). Remove (outlined danger, secondary): Delete event only for an empty setup with no protected history; Cancel event (reason; history kept; players notified). Super Admin: Hide/Restore (rules: §4); Hide is offered on legacy Finished events.
- **Lifecycle action outcomes.** Pending: "Working…", the dialog holds focus and blocks dismissal. Failed: inputs kept, Try again. Stale: the version read at dialog open is checked; if the action still applies the consequences refresh and confirmation is asked again, naming the latest change as "‹audit action label› (‹actor›)"; if it no longer applies it says so and offers only Close. Uncertain: fields lock, the button becomes Check again and reports what the event shows; closing re-reads the page. Success: toast, the page updates to the new phase, focus moves to the stage heading. Dialogs and evidence codes post with JSON outcomes (applied/stale/invalid/refused) and re-read with no-store `GET ?handler=Current`; plain form posts keep post-redirect-get.
- **At a glance:** participants (or teams and players), board, approved submissions, uploads (open/closed/reopened with time), evidence codes, WOM; labels link to owning pages; nothing in Needs attention or requirements is repeated.
- **Evidence codes** (rules: §11): turn on/off, code history (active, scheduled, ended), add a code (generate, active from, optional note) in a dialog. Overview also keeps upload recovery. Date/time inputs (reopen uploads, resume end, code "Active from") use the shared picker; a typed invalid entry counts as that field's error.
- **Public links** follow publication facts, not phase. Never public (`FirstPublicAt` unset): a plain note, no links; a never-public cancelled event shows no panel; every public route returns not found. Permanent link `/Events/{slug}/Signups` whenever the event has been public, with a note on what visitors get (published board/results → board; else active published roster → teams; else signup list; admins always see the signup list). Signup form `/Events/{slug}/Signup` only while signups are accepted (open, future close) and nothing is published yet. Teams link whenever an active roster publication exists; Board link whenever the board is published, including before Live. Cancelled and previously public: only the permanent link, noted "Visitors see that the event was cancelled"; /Signups, /Teams and /Board stay reachable with a cancelled notice; Stats returns not found. Stats is not listed separately (reached from the board). Fields show the path; Copy copies the absolute URL.
- **Hidden event (Super Admin).** The limited view opens on the plain event URL (legacy `?hidden=true` accepted), reached from the Events Hidden view; it shows lifecycle information and the retained lifecycle/quarantine history and replaces the public-link panel; only Restore can be posted; no workspace or other mutation. Ordinary Admins get Not Found.
- **States:** loading skeleton; failed read with Try again; an unknown or inaccessible event id returns the shared Admin 404 page; read-only results (Archived); cancelled state with the private reason.
- **URL:** links push event sub-page URLs; Back closes an open dialog first; a pending action blocks leaving.

### Identity

Route `/Admin/Events/Identity/{id}`. A form card without a rail (no drawer) editing the event's public details (rules: §5). It does not hold banner/artwork (retired), dates (Schedule), lifecycle actions or readiness (Overview).

- **Fields:** name; description (optional, 4,000); buy-in information (optional, 2,000; free text explaining the entry payment, not a price or payment); timezone. Description and buy-in accept over-limit typing with counter and error; the server refusal stays authoritative.
- **Timezone select** shows zone ID with current UTC offset; choices are the supported list (Europe/Copenhagen, UTC). A stored zone no longer offered is shown as "· current" and kept while other fields save; once changed away it cannot be chosen again. Before public exposure a change saves normally with a note under the field. After exposure the review dialog asks "Show this event's times in ‹zone›?" with Cancel and "Save with ‹zone›", naming both zones, one row per scheduled moment and one "Not scheduled yet: …" line for unset moments (or "Nothing is scheduled yet, so no shown times change.").
- **Permanent link:** shows and copies the absolute URL of `/Events/{slug}/Signups`, built from the server slug and the current site origin; never changes with the name; no slug editor. Other public links and onward Teams/Board routing stay on Overview.
- **Saving:** explicit Save changes (no autosave), aria-disabled with nothing to save; while saving, fields and Save are disabled and navigation waits. Validation errors stay with their fields (summary when several fail), focus the first, keep values; a failed save shows an error banner and keeps edits. Success and no-change stay on Identity (post-redirect-get) with a toast and a quiet Saved state, without adding a history entry.
- **Conflicts:** the form keeps original field baselines and timezone-review state; a field another admin changed keeps the draft and shows the current value with Use theirs; saving after review keeps mine for unresolved choices (merge rule: §5).
- **Uncertain save:** fields lock and Save becomes Check again; the full expected tuple (name, description, buy-in, timezone) is frozen separately from the edit intent; the uncertain response renders the posted draft without a database read. The no-store readback returns event id, values and event version: matching values with a moved version show "Up to date" (not proof this request saved); a moved version with different values enters the conflict flow; an unchanged version means not applied and the draft is kept; a failed or malformed read stays uncertain. No automatic retry or receipt. Leaving offers Check again (default) or Leave anyway, warning the change may already have happened. A lost session keeps and shows the unsaved input before sign-in. A route refusal is never success.
- **Read-only:** Live events keep identity and timezone read-only (the Live end correction is on Schedule). Finished, Archived and Cancelled events show a read-only page with one sentence explaining why; read-only values keep full text colour.

### Schedule

Route `/Admin/Events/Schedule/{id}`. Edits five times: signups open, signups close, team draft (optional), event starts, event ends (rules: §5). No capacity or automatic-opening toggle; it links to Signup setup (capacity, access, form), Identity (timezone) and Overview (open/close/reopen signups, start/end/resume) without repeating them.

- **Layout:** one form card with sections Signups (open, close), Team draft, Event (start, end). Below the end a derived line shows uploads close at end + 30 minutes (not a setting). Summary is one item: "Times are in ‹timezone›; change it on Identity. Open, close or start by hand on Overview." with both links; no event-stage sentence.
- **Picker:** every time uses the shared picker in the event timezone with five-minute steps, posting event-local values; the server owns DST and step validation. Unchanged instants stay exact; ambiguous or skipped changed local times are rejected; the picker fits short viewports.
- **Hints:** each field has a short hint. Overdue start: "This start time has passed and the event hasn't started. Choose a new time, or start it on Overview." Scheduled starting is described as conditional on readiness, never promised. An older schedule with automatic opening off stays off when other fields change and says so ("Planned only…"); choosing a new opening time turns it on and the hint updates as you type.
- **Locked fields** show the time as read-only text with the reason in place, e.g. "Signups opened 1 Jun 2027, 18:00.", "Locked while the team draft is underway.", "Signups closed … To set a new closing time, reopen signups on Overview." Live allows changing only the end.
- **Unknown stored timezone IDs** are retained; Schedule and Signup setup label dates as UTC fallback and link to Identity; Schedule stays read-only until the timezone is supported. A page initialization failure shows page recovery (no connection toast) and releases navigation.
- **Save:** one Save for the whole schedule; a private draft saves directly. A public event with opening, closing, start or end changed shows a wide confirmation of each changed time before/after in the event timezone with UTC offset, plus the new upload deadline if the end changes; a draft-time-only change has no confirmation. A Live end change confirms before/after end and upload deadline and needs a reason (2,000 characters). Cancelling a confirmation keeps every entered value including the reason. Success stays on Schedule.
- **Errors:** lifecycle and order errors map to field errors; an overlapping public window is a form error; the exact-WOM-window refusal shows on Event start and Event end without rounding the configured instants.
- **Recovery:** unchanged fields come from the version-checked row, not reparsed from the minute display; the overdue automatic-opening exception and legacy disabled state are kept. Stale: untouched fields take theirs, both-changed keep yours with Use theirs. The no-store Current read exposes the full precise schedule (six values, no capacity), automatic-opening state, version, timezone, phase and editability; matching values = "Up to date" (not proof); unchanged or competing state keeps the submission and uncertainty; a failed read = unknown. Check again is read-only. Failed saves keep times. Discard and uncertain-leave confirmations guard navigation.
- **States:** loading skeleton; failed read with Try again. Back/Forward closes an open confirmation or picker first.

### Signup setup

Schedule owns dates, Overview owns readiness and opening/closing, Participants owns registered people. Summary is only "Dates are on Schedule; opening and closing signups on Overview." with both links. `/Admin/Events/Questions` GET redirects to the Signup form tab.

- **Tabs:** Settings and Signup form; `?tab=form` replaces the URL (Back leaves the page, not the tab). Unsaved edits stay with their tab, which shows a dot; leaving with unsaved edits asks first.
- **Settings** has two cards, Capacity and Signup code, each with its own Save (separate operations).

They're told their place is confirmed." and the button reads "Save and confirm N"; raising with nobody waiting shows how many places open; lowering says no one loses their place and new signups join the waiting list once full. The success toast reports the number the server actually promoted.
- **Signup code:** "Require a signup code" toggle. With a stored code the card says "A code is set" and never shows or copies it (stored as a hash); the field becomes "New code" and may be left empty to keep the current one. Turning protection off warns that the stored code is deleted and a new one is needed to turn it on again. The card states signup codes are not the evidence codes managed on Overview.

Responses show the submitted baseline separately from current settings.
- **Signup form sections:** Playing accounts (first account, then optional extra fields; they count toward account/EHB rules), Alt accounts (information only, no EHB, never on teams, evidence or board), Captains (required yes/no captain volunteer and optional co-captain field), Custom questions. Each row shows label, answer type, required/optional, then choices or help text, and answer counts once players have signed up. Only custom questions are numbered and reorderable (up/down one step); section text says other fields keep their places. Standard fields (first playing account, captain volunteer) are marked "Always asked" with no actions. The form states public visibility: playing accounts appear on the public signup list with EHB, alt accounts without EHB, captain volunteer and custom answers are public, co-captain answers are not shown.
- **Account fields:** Add creates the field in one step with its default label and opens inline rename with the label selected; Rename edits the label only; Delete… opens a counted confirmation. **Co-captain:** Turn on applies immediately; Turn off… opens a counted confirmation. **Custom questions:** Add question and Edit open the question drawer (form visible behind); Delete… opens a counted confirmation. Label edits and reordering never ask for confirmation.
- **Question drawer:** question (required, 300 characters), help text (1,000), answer type (Text, Number, Yes or no, Single choice), choices via a list editor for Single choice (Enter adds the next; at least one, unique ignoring case, blank rows dropped, 4,000 characters total), "Players must answer".
- **After the first response** a banner says answer types, choices and required settings are locked, labels and help text can still change, custom questions can still be reordered, and new questions are optional. Editing shows type, choices and required read-only, each with its reason (change type = delete and add new); adding forces the question optional, explaining earlier players won't have an answer.
- **Question drawer outcomes:** failed keeps entries; stale refreshes the form behind, keeps entries and saving again works; gone (question deleted) saves nothing and offers only Close; draft started saves nothing; players signed up meanwhile resets structural settings to stored values and keeps label and help text. Uncertain locks the fields; Check again reads the form back: for an add it looks for a new question with the same label and, if found, says "A question called X is now on the form. It may have come from your request", offers Close and focuses that row's Edit; otherwise says it wasn't added and keeps entries. A retry never silently duplicates: adds carry a request ID (rules: §6), and a field created then deleted is reported removed, never recreated.
- **Add request IDs:** an identical retry of a custom-question or account-field add returns the created field without another write, even after later form changes; a reused ID with changed intent, baseline, actor or event fails closed without disclosing the prior result; every attempt rechecks Admin authority, event visibility and pre-draft state; the submitted request is kept while uncertain.
- **Counted confirmations** (delete custom question, delete account field, turn off co-captain) state how many saved answers are removed; for account fields how many event account registrations are released; that players keep accounts saved on their profiles; that signups, participant status and other answers are unchanged; that it can't be undone (co-captain: can be turned on again but removed answers don't return). If counts change before confirming nothing happens; the dialog shows new numbers against old and asks again. Outcomes: gone, failed, uncertain; after uncertain, Check again reports it is still on the form (with current numbers) or no longer on it.
- **Immediate operations** (add account field, rename, move, co-captain on): failure shows a toast and changes nothing; uncertain shows a form-level banner with "Check the form" that re-reads and reports what the form shows (never claims success), and Add buttons stay disabled until then; a stale move refreshes the order and asks to move again if needed. All form mutations send the form version; refreshing another card never rebases a pending uncertain operation.
- **Read-only:** once the team draft starts, or for a cancelled event, the page is read-only with a banner giving the reason (e.g. "The team draft started on 26 May…") and no controls. On Cancelled, Finalized or Archived events the Questions route allows only exact replay of an already committed add; every other change redirects to Overview with "This event is read-only in its current lifecycle state." Imported events without a signup form (also after reopen or finalize) show stored Settings read-only and "No signup form was recorded for this imported event." on the form tab; nothing historical is created.
- **States:** loading skeleton; failed read with Try again; empty alt accounts and custom questions; unset capacity "Not set". Back/Forward closes an open drawer or confirmation first and waits while a request runs; leaving with an unconfirmed outcome offers Check again or Leave anyway.

### Participants

Route `/Admin/Events/Participants/{id}`. The single workspace for confirmed, waiting and withdrawn participants, including private payment and notes (rules: §6). Admins manage the signup pool in event Draft, Signup open or Signup closed while the team draft is unlocked; on later and terminal events the retained detail stays reachable for payment and note maintenance only, other controls being lifecycle-gated.

- **List:** all statuses with identity, source, queue position, regular/alt accounts, EHB, private paid state and team state. Search covers all account RSNs, username and Discord name. A Captain column (Yes/No; a co-captain shown as "with ‹Name›" on a second line) sits before Team and sorts with it. Manual-team members show their team, and waiting positions follow the capacity rule that manual teams never free a place.
- **Drawer:** "Discord: Linked / Not linked", never the Discord display name. Source shows "Website signup / CSV import / Added by an admin"; no numeric ID beside the website account. "Signup answers" lists every custom question as question: answer, editable until the draft starts, then read-only; answers to retired questions show read-only and are never saved; there is no participant's note field or row flag. Informational account slots: one slot labelled "Alt account", several each labelled by question, none hides the section; a typed RSN is accepted as an event-only account with hint "Not one of @user's saved accounts. Used for this event only." and never saved to the player. A withdrawn participant's accounts and answers are read-only ("Restore to edit"); payment and private note stay editable.
- **Add:** search by username, Discord name or RSN (the results show the Discord name); no recently-joined list. Picks an existing active account and its saved playing accounts with stored EHB (no WOM call), primary and payment (default Unpaid); no code, questionnaire, captain role or notes; captain volunteering defaults to No; other answers are not fabricated; placement uses normal capacity or the explicit add-one-place confirmation (rules: §6).
- **Actions:** one confirmed "Withdraw participant" for any reason (record moves to the Withdrawn section; automatic history; no reason). "Move to waiting list" stays visible but disabled, with an explanation, while confirmed places are available; likewise Restore's Waiting choice. With unsaved drawer edits, Withdraw… / Restore… first ask to discard. The UI confirms capacity expansion and membership loss before submitting. Promotion shows in the toast: "X withdrawn · Y confirmed from the waiting list"; after Move to waiting list "… · Y confirmed".
- **Outcomes:** a definite refusal shows the server reason and keeps edits; a lost response re-reads (`GET ?handler=Current`) and says "We couldn't confirm whether your changes were saved …" with current details; Add and row actions never resubmit after a lost response. The persisted result is displayed. Corrections after signups close reuse signup validation and reservations without changing queue position or status.
- **URL:** Add leaves no history entry (`?add=1` is replaced); Back/Forward reopen the participant drawer; stale list data is re-read behind an open drawer without a discard prompt.

### Teams / Draft

Route `/Admin/Events/Draft/{id}`. Team setup, the live website draft, manual rosters and finalized corrections (rules: §7).

- **Setup (2+ drafted teams):** team cards and participant search, no participant table or affiliation/image editing. Readiness card: signups closed (link to Overview), every drafted team has a Captain (with Assign), team sizes work out, a size summary such as "44 players across 4 drafted teams: 4 teams of 11" noting non-drafted manual teams, and "Set the event end in Schedule" with link when the configured end is missing or past. Start draft… is unavailable until every check passes.
- **Team cards:** name, "Website draft" or "Manual roster", size (with final size for drafted teams), EHB total, members with role badges (C, CC) and EHB, warning line (no captain, too many preassigned). Footer: Assign captain (while none) and Preassign player or Add member. Team menu: Edit team… (name 30 characters, unique; "Takes part in the website draft"), Add captain… (always; several Captains allowed), Remove team… with confirmation. Removing a populated team: "Its {0} members go back to having no team. Nothing about the players changes; they stay signed up."; without confirmation intent the server refuses "{0} now has members. Nothing was removed; check the team before trying again." Member menu: Make captain, Make co-captain, Make participant, Move to another manual-roster team, Remove from team.
- **Participant selector drawer:** searches by primary account, shows EHB and a "Captain volunteer" chip; assigning a captain lists volunteers first and shows other teams' members disabled; adding a member asks the role (Participant, Captain, Co-captain); volunteering never assigns a role.
- **Manual rosters (0–1 drafted teams):** readiness covers closed signups and at least one member per team (plus the event end check); unplaced confirmed players are a non-blocking note ("4 confirmed players aren't on a team…"); Finalize rosters… publishes directly with no draft run or fabricated picks, and its confirmation states the unplaced count and that they can be added later as corrections.
- **Starting:** Start draft… confirms team sizes and what locks, then starts and takes control. Draw order is a separate step in the turn strip, with Redraw in the draft menu until the first pick is recorded (that marker persists even if picks are undone). The draw animates waiting then the order, with reduced-motion support and no early success.
- **Running workspace:** sidebar and header compact (sidebar auto-collapses; the admin's own toggle is kept until the draft ends). Team columns show actual members and picks with current/target counts, no open-place rows; the pool shrinks as columns grow; primary account and EHB readable; all teams visible on desktop; bounded pool scrolling for large drafts. Click or Enter drafts immediately (no selection step or confirmation); while pending the chip and a team row spin and the pool is inert so nothing is sent twice; a confirmed pick uses the shared row entrance and flash and focus moves to the next chip. Undo removes only the latest active pick and names it (`pickId`); a different latest pick is refused: "The latest pick changed. Nothing was undone; the current board is shown."
- **Draft menu:** Draw or Redraw order, Preassign a player… (until the first pick, after Start, before or after drawing; for a confirmed participant not on a team, before event start; counts toward team size and can change whose turn is next; afterwards shown unavailable with the reason), Finalize draft… (once every player is placed), Cancel draft…. Cancel with active picks explains picks are never removed and must be undone one at a time first, with no confirm button; with none it returns to setup keeping teams, captains and preassigned members, says whether the drawn order is kept, and releases control.
- **Draft control chip:** "You have control" (Release), "Another admin has control" (Take over…), "No one has control" (Take control). The workspace never names the other admin or a last-action time; only the take-over confirmation names them. Losing control (takeover or refused pick) makes the pool inert with a banner: "Another admin took control of the draft." or "Your control lapsed."; a lost live connection shows "Live updates stopped. … Control lapses 5 minutes after its last renewal." with Reload. Control renews every 120 seconds while held. The controller's picks arrive live for everyone: a realtime change triggers a re-read (not while a command or check is pending); realtime is invalidation only.
- **Finalize** confirmation says it publishes rosters and draft results (only rosters for manual rosters), doesn't publish the board or start the event, allows individual corrections until first Live, and can't be reopened. Finalized rosters list drafted teams in draw order. The admin stays on Teams / Draft; header and toast offer Open Board only when a board exists, otherwise an inert header button with "No board yet. Create one on the Board page."
- **Finalized corrections (before first Live):** banner explains limits; Add member selects a signed-up player or "Other website accounts" (playing account and EHB), Participant role only, saved with "Add and republish"; Remove asks first, stating the new size, that the original pick stays in history, that removing a Captain leaves the team without one, and that moving means adding on the other team afterwards; role changes and renames available; tags show pick number, Pre or Added.
- **Republish and WOM lines:** roster publication is separate from WOM outcomes. Only a WOM operation created at or after the publication counts as current (never by update time); queued, failed or unknown never means synced. Corrections and finalize show "Rosters republished." only for a newer cycle than shown, then a separate WOM line (went through / waiting to be sent / failed / isn't known / couldn't be queued; none without a managed group), never provider text.
- **Live:** membership locked; banner says Captain/Co-captain roles can still change; member menus offer only Make captain/co-captain/participant; the team menu offers Add captain… for a current member. Role changes stay available until the submission window closes. Awaiting final review and terminal events: same workspace, rosters read-only with "Rosters are final." / "This event is finished/cancelled/archived. Rosters are shown as they were."; in final review the role menu stays only while uploads are open. A historical paused draft shows "Historical paused draft. Pausing was retired, so this draft is read-only and no roster changes are available."
- **Outcomes:** every change has saving, failed (toast or in-dialog banner) and stale ("another admin changed this team first" reloads the roster) states. Commands keep their intended IDs and fields and verify by no-store readback (pick by participant, Undo by the shown latest pick, draw by positions, control by controller, team/member/role by id); matching pick numbers or order never prove a timed-out result; e.g. "A team named X now exists. It isn't known whether this request created it." Uncertain doesn't update the view; a Check again banner blocks repeats; a failed read stays unknown; no automatic replay. Leaving while saving waits, while uncertain asks (Check again / Leave anyway). A typed team name or chosen player asks before discard.
- **States:** loading skeleton, failed load with Try again, empty event. Back/Forward closes dialog, drawer or menu first.

### Board

Route `/Admin/Events/Board/{id}`. The board is the main workspace, with detailed editing in one drawer beside it (rules: §8; EHB: §10). Catalogue owns bosses, activities, drops, rates and GP; Board only links to it.

- **Header:** status badge (Draft · private, Approved · private, Published, Correction · private); size (with Resize) and filled positions; a one-line state note (when approved/published and what returns it to draft); editing-control chip; Preview; one primary action (Approve board, Publish board… or Publish correction…); menu (Board size…, Return to draft, Correct published board…, Discard correction…).
- **Tiles** show name, effective EHB and flags: artwork, "Manual" (EHB override in use), "2 parts" (several objectives), clipboard (manual challenge), lock (evidence submitted), warning (no estimate), dot (changed in the open correction). Empty positions read "Add tile" ("Empty" in view mode).
- **Line totals:** row totals at row ends, column totals below. Complete lines 25% or more from the average line are marked ↑ or ↓ with the percentage; incomplete lines are muted; marks are advisory, never a blocker. Hovering or focusing a row/column total highlights its tiles with more than colour (outline and label).
- **Moving tiles:** drag onto another position, or keyboard (arrows move between positions, M picks up, arrows choose the target, Enter places, Escape cancels, with an explanatory bar); empty target = move, occupied = swap; totals recalculate immediately; tiles animate to new places (instant with reduced motion). Enter edits or adds; Delete removes with confirmation.
- **Resize** dialog with steppers 1–8 describes the result as you go ("Adds 11 empty positions. Every tile keeps its row and column."); shrinking past occupied positions is blocked, naming the tiles to move or remove; on an approved board it says the board returns to draft.
- **Planning figures:** total board EHB, players per team (manually adjustable, still after rosters finalize, planning only, no extra explanation), EHB per player, per player per day (with event length), line spread (absolute and %). "More figures": tiles filled, average tile, lowest/highest line, tiles without estimate, event length. No actual-team EHB breakdown.
- **Tile editor drawer** (create, edit, view). Name falls back to suggested source names. Description is the automatic text with "Write your own"; an override offers "Use automatic description"; after edits the automatic description reads "Written automatically … when you save" (the server recomputes). Artwork upload/replace/remove (PNG, JPEG, WebP). While a published board is being corrected the eyebrow reads "Working copy".
- **Objectives:** kind chosen once per tile (Collect drops or Manual challenge); switching keeps the other kind's entries until save. A single objective has no card chrome; several become numbered collapsible cards with their own description and estimate and "All 2 must be completed". Drop objective: searchable multi-select of bosses/activities as chips; eligible drops grouped by source (sources with chosen drops show only those, with "Show all"; new sources show all), each with its rate or "No rate"; "Collect [n] of the chosen drops" with a one-line counting summary and Change, revealing "The same drop can count more than once" and "Some drops count as more than one" (adds a "counts as" field per chosen drop). Manual challenge: "What must be completed?" and "Complete [n] times"; evidence and approval still required.
- **Estimate:** drop tiles show the server's calculated EHB for stored objectives and "Calculated when you save" after objective changes (no client calculation), or plain reasons it can't be calculated (missing catalogue rate, linking to Catalogue, from catalogue data; target unreachable without repeats). "Set EHB manually" adds an event-tile-only override with the calculated value struck through and "Use calculated" to return. Manual challenges require an EHB estimate.
- **Validation on save** focuses the first problem: target 1–10,000; at least one source and drop; weights 1–10,000; manual text required; EHB above 0 up to 100,000; description 4,000 characters; decimals normalized to one place, junk rejected.
- **Evidence locks:** locked objectives show the reason in place, and their sources, drops, target and counting can't change; kind, override and adding/removing objectives are locked for that tile; Remove tile is replaced by "Can't be removed: evidence was submitted"; name, description and artwork stay editable. Locks from late evidence survive correction publish or discard.
- **Editor footer:** Remove tile…, unsaved-changes indicator, Cancel, Save; Ctrl/⌘+Enter saves. View mode (someone else editing, or published board) is the same drawer read-only.
- **Approve board** runs without confirmation and validates the whole board; failures appear as one list with links opening each tile: empty positions grouped as one "(N empty)" item that jumps to and highlights the first, drop tile without automatic EHB (naming the missing rates), manual tile without EHB, drops without a catalogue GP value. Issue codes without page wording show the server's text. On an approved board any tile, position or size change returns it to draft; the editor says so before saving and the toast confirms; Return to draft is in the menu.
- **Publish board…** requires finalized rosters, event not started and end in the future; when blocked the button explains why and a banner links to Teams / Draft; one confirmation says publishing doesn't start the event; Publish returns every refusal together. A published board is read-only with Correct published board… in the menu.
- **Correction:** Correct published board… needs a reason (2,000 characters) and the dialog is the confirmation (no checkbox). During correction a banner shows the reason and evidence rule, changed tiles carry a dot, and approval shows the working copy. Publish correction… validates and replaces the public board; its confirmation says "N tiles differ from the published board, M of them removed." from the server count and repeats the reason. Discard correction… restores the working copy from the published board without touching results or evidence.
- **Preview** opens a modal labelled with what it shows (current draft, frozen approved version, what players see now, or your private correction); opening changes nothing. It shows the board as players see it on the team board (tile art, names, points) for the version being viewed — the board only (no sidebar, header, stats or team progress), tiles not interactive. Tiles without their own image show boss artwork by the shared rule (§8); a published board uses the same public tile image players load. The legacy BoardPreview route redirects to Board.
- **Editing control:** no presence list or live re-read. Chip: "You're editing" (Stop), "<name> is editing" (Take over…), "Viewing" (Start editing). Take-over confirmation is titled "Take over editing?", names the other admin only in the text, and says their unsaved edits stay in their browser. While holding the lease, page or drawer activity renews it via `POST ?handler=RenewEditing` at most once a minute (no version or audit change). Losing control while the editor is open keeps entries in a read-only drawer with Take over…; retaking makes them editable again; a dirty draft and its version survive takeover with no silent overwrite. Another admin's change surfaces on the next action as a stale refusal with the draft kept.
- **Transport:** commands from script answer in place (JSON outcome + no-store readback); non-script posts keep post-redirect-get. Save, publish and discard keep their full intent and read back truthfully when uncertain.
- **States:** loading skeleton and failed load with Try again (also in the drawer for a failed editor load); empty and partly built boards; saving feedback uses the shared minimum, moves/team size/editing control the short one; failures in dialog or toast; "Another admin changed the board first" keeps entries and loads the latest board; uncertain waits for Check again. Unsaved tile changes ask before discard; an uncertain change asks before leaving.
- **Terminal events:** page, editor data and readback load read-only on Cancelled, Finalized and Archived; every post is refused; no working artwork, retained published artwork still shows; a terminal event without a board shows "No board recorded." and never creates one.
- **URL:** an open tile adds `?tile=B3` (row letter, column number). Direct `?tile=` opens that tile after loading. A missing tile shows "That tile isn't on this board" on the page, "This tile isn't on the board any more" in an open drawer. Back/Forward closes the top dialog, drawer or menu first and restores the correct layer.

### Review

Routes `/Admin/Review` (queue for the selected event) and `/Admin/Review/Details/{id}` (submission workspace); search and status travel with the submission URL. Review checks evidence and decides on it (rules: §11); it has no finalization controls or readiness summary (Final review is separate).

- **Event:** the queue opens for the selected non-hidden event in any state, even empty; the event comes only from the event selector, never from team/tile dropdowns. No `eventId`: "Choose an event"; hidden or unknown `eventId`: 404 "This event isn't available", never silently another event's queue. Details derives the event from the submission (a link with another event is corrected); unknown id or hidden event: 404 "This submission isn't available" with a way back.
- **Times** lead with UTC; the secondary time is in the event's timezone named by city, in the queue's Uploaded column and workspace facts; header "Times in UTC, with {city} time below".
- **Queue header:** "Review", pending count, event state ("Live · ends …" or "Ended … · uploads closed, review continues"; terminal "Finished/Archived/Cancelled · review is read-only").
- **Search and status:** live search across team, credited account and tile without reloads; status as a segmented filter with counts (All, Pending, Approved, Rejected, Withdrawn, Reversed), a menu on phones; both replace the URL (no entry per keystroke). Order: Pending first, newest first within Pending and within the rest.
- **Queue columns:** Uploaded (pinned on horizontal scroll), Team, Account, Tile (with drop and boss, or "Manual objective"), Status, Check (short warnings After end, Paused period, Same image, No screenshot, Left team). The expected code is not a column. One after-end boundary (actual end, else configured end) is used in queue and workspace.
- **Queue states:** "No submissions yet" (with when evidence can arrive) distinct from "No submissions match" (Clear search and status); terminal empty "No evidence was submitted in {event}."; loading skeleton; load failure with Try again keeping search and status.
- **Workspace** is one page (no drawer): header with back to Review, tile name with status, and Previous / position / Next within the list you came from (its order at opening).
- **Viewer:** inline evidence viewer with Fit, 100%, zoom level, keyboard/pointer gestures and load-failure retry; Original opens `/Evidence/{assetId}`.
- **Checks** in the side column, phrased as checks: uploaded after the end (shows "Submission time", minutes after the end and "Latest clan event time:" with the end in UTC; grace uploads are allowed but the drop must be before the end); paused interval ("Check the in-game time: the event was paused.", judged from the screenshot's game time); credited player left the team ("‹account› left ‹team› at ‹time›. Drops before that time count for the team; later ones don't.", refreshed after a credited-account correction); same image on other submissions (with links; "reject it with a reason", no mark-duplicate action); no screenshot. No active-account-since or switched-from content.
- **Facts to compare with the screenshot:** Account (team; latest relevant UTC account change, full swap history only in audit), Drop (and boss), Objective, Code (retained code, only when codes are enabled), Uploaded (UTC first, event-timezone below), Event window (actual start/end in UTC, paused interval), Contribution (what approving adds, partial amounts, progress, or what was added/removed). Tile name only in the header. Submitter (Captain) note and reviewer feedback only when present; no empty placeholders.
- **Contribution wording:** cap-limited with room: "Approving adds {Add}" + "Worth {W}, but the drop's limit allows only {Add} more."; limit reached with work left: "The drop's limit is reached" + "This drop can't add more to the objective ({used} of {target}). If the submission doesn't count, reject it with a reason."; objective complete: "Nothing left to add". Approve stays enabled when nothing is left or there is no screenshot (a warning shows) and the server refuses on submit. Reversed shows "Removed N" and the actual reallocation. Values come from the same allocation path as approval.
- **Collapsed below:** History (decisions, corrections, reasons, newest first; stored audit wording; approvals show their stored detail line such as "Approved contribution: 1." as the reason line), Earlier approvals for this objective (same team, links), Files (current and earlier versions, size, dimensions, upload time, checksum, Open).
- **Decision panel**, pinned at the bottom of the side column (sticky at the screen bottom on narrow layouts). Pending: Approve (direct, no confirmation); Reject… (inline required reason, 4,000 characters, says who is notified); Correct details… (tile, drop and credited account in a card above the facts; new weight shown; reason required; "Change at least one detail" if nothing changed). The correction objective picker lists options per objective grouped by tile under "Objective"; the account picker includes released playing accounts and former team members marked Released / Left team / Current, never informational accounts; the participant follows the account; a tile with several objectives distinguishes them; a completed manual objective refuses correction like other types. Approved: who approved and when, and Reverse approval… confirming contribution removal, progress recalculation, that later evidence may take up the amount and that the record stays in history; reason required. Rejected/Withdrawn/Reversed: the attempt is history and a new upload is a separate submission; no reapproval.
- **Closed states:** Draft/Signups "Review starts when the event is Live."; Finalized "{event} is finished, so review is read-only."; Archived "{event} is archived, so review is read-only."; Cancelled "{event} was cancelled, so review is read-only."; Approve, Reject, Correct, Reverse and Contribution are hidden.
- **Blocked approval** shows "Waiting for an earlier upload" with "Open the earlier upload ({UTC})", keeping queue search and status.
- **Decision outcomes:** one request at a time (pressed button spins, every decision control disabled, leaving waits; shared minimum durations). Decisions answer in place (saved/refused/stale/blocked). Success: result card "Approved" + "{N} added to {team}." (e.g. "Approved · 2 added to Team Masori") with Next submission and Back to the queue, focus to the outcome, toast. Failure: "Couldn't approve this submission. Nothing changed. Try again."; definite refusal "Couldn't {action}." + server reason. Stale (another admin decided or the team withdrew): nothing saved, current record shown, banner names who changed it, unsaved reason kept if still pending. Uncertain: banner with Check status; decisions disabled until checked; the dialog never closes on backdrop or Escape; Check status reports "It wasn't saved. The submission is unchanged, so you can decide again." or "We couldn't confirm your request, but the submission is now {status}."; never infers who acted from status alone; the readback returns current status and the latest review attribution, not a receipt; a failed read stays unknown; nothing is replayed. Non-uncertain notices have Dismiss. Leaving with an unsaved reason or correction asks first; backdrop never means Leave anyway.
- **URL:** opening a submission pushes its URL with search/status; Back returns to the queue with them and focuses that row, briefly highlighted; header Review steps back when the previous entry is that queue, otherwise opens it; Previous/Next replace the URL.

### Final review

Route `/Admin/Events/Finalize/{id}`. Shows standings, resolves publication blockers and publishes official results; afterwards shows official results and publication history (rules: §13). Evidence decisions belong to Review.

- **Header:** state badge (Provisional, Provisional · correction, Official · version n, Live, or the pre-live phase); end and upload-close times or publication time; times in the event timezone.
- **Publication card:** headline "n things to resolve before publishing", "Waiting for uploads to close" or "Ready to publish", with what publishing does ("makes these standings official as version n and archives the event"); in a correction cycle says when and by whom results were reopened and which version stays in history. Publish official results… is unavailable, with the reason beside it, until every requirement is met.
- **Requirements**, each with state and destination: in final review; uploads closed (an open window is an expected timed wait, e.g. "They close at 10:10 (in 10 min)… this is expected"); no pending submissions (Open Review to the Pending-filtered queue); placements calculated from the published board (Open Board otherwise); every other applicable server blocker (e.g. submission cutoff required, final-review cycle missing). No "another event is current" row (server guards remain). Nothing can be acknowledged or overridden.
- **WOM notes:** only unsuccessful refresh outcomes per version are shown; the next eligible time is never displayed. A pending or rejected end update adds the last-pre-end-data warning to Publish, never a blocker.
- **Standings:** place, team, full board (completion time), lines, tiles, credited EHB, score reached ("At completion" for full boards). Each row says what separates it from its neighbour and highlights that value. Exact ties share a place shown "=3" (numbering 1, 2, 3, 3, 5); names only order tied rows; ranks are computed before the top-three slice. "How placements are decided" explains the event's placement rule and tie rule. Provisional standings are calculated; official ones are the stored snapshot. Team column pinned on horizontal scroll.
- **Official results stamp:** version, time, admin, "The event is archived", and any retained WOM refresh note.
- **Publication history:** each version (Official or Retained) and each reopening with admin, time and reason; View results opens a version's stored results unchanged in a drawer.
- **Correct official results:** Reopen results… or the reason it isn't available; when another event is current the button is disabled with state-specific guidance (Live / Awaiting final review / legacy Finalized, e.g. "<event> is the current event. Archive it before reopening these results.") and an "Open event" link.
- **Publish confirmation:** the top three as they will be published (complete tie data) and what happens (official version n, archived in the same step, WOM refresh and what a skip means, participants notified); one confirmation, no reason. **Reopen confirmation:** returns the event to provisional final review and makes it current again; the version stays in history and the next publication creates a new one; doesn't restart the event or reopen uploads; reason required (2,000 characters).
- **Outcomes:** published → official view, focus moves, toast. Skipped or failed refresh → the same plus a warning banner saying results are published and why the refresh didn't run (never a failed publication). Failed → dialog stays with "Nothing changed. Try again." Stale → nothing done, current state with banner and changed rows highlighted; stale archived state is distinguished from an actual reopening. Access removed → dialog says so, only Close. Uncertain → banner; Check status reads state back ("The results are official: version 2 was published at 10:04…" / "The results weren't published. Nothing changed."), never claiming no historical change from a negative read and never attributing the state; publish and reopen unavailable until checked; reload or returning is also a readback. The pending intent and reason draft survive reload per event and guard navigation.
- **Other states:** loading skeleton; load failure with Try again; Live ("Final review starts when the event ends", live standings); no calculated results (pre-live or no teams on the published board) with Open Board. Legacy Finalized records stay readable (not shown as a normal state).
- **URL:** switching events stays on Final review; Open Review and Open Board push their pages; View results pushes `?version=n` (Back closes, Forward reopens, Close/Escape step back); a direct link opens the version over the page, and a failed load retry restores it; unknown version "This version isn't available"; invalid value dropped. Dialogs are not in the URL; Back closes an idle dialog first and waits while a request runs.

### Wise Old Man

Route `/Admin/Events/WiseOldMan/{id}`. Answers first whether the connection works, how current the data is and whether an admin must act; diagnostics and occasional management below (rules: §14). The website's dates are always the schedule; the page never edits dates or rosters. Loading or reloading reads stored state and never contacts Wise Old Man.

- **Header:** "Wise Old Man", state badge (Connected, Needs attention, Not connected), read-only phase when relevant ("Inspection only: the event is archived"), "The website's dates are the schedule", times in the event timezone. Cancelled and Finalized events show read-only text.
- **Main issue banner**, one, above everything, by priority: unknown operation outcome; queued deletion; conflict (changed on WOM, missing, shared with another event); rejected management code (Replace code…); missing competition (last data kept; in final review "Final review and publishing can continue"); rate limiting or unavailability (last data with automatic retry time); data more than an hour old. An unmatched WOM end uses this banner and the Updates card with the exact target; only a stored matching end-update operation can be retried; fetching pauses; publishing stays available; no restart after publication or reopening. Other sections refer to the banner rather than repeating it.
- **Connected competition:** number, title, Open on Wise Old Man, capability chip with one line: website-created ("Created by the website, which keeps it up to date."), linked with a working management code ("Made elsewhere. The website can update it but cannot delete it."), linked read-only ("Made elsewhere. The website reads its data but doesn't change it."), unknown origin ("Unknown origin; contact an operator", locked). Capability and deletion eligibility come from the actual credential and service, never from origin alone.
- **Three facts:** Latest data (last successful fetch, how long ago, WOM's own update time), Accounts found (matched of expected playing accounts, participants separately), Next scheduled fetch (or automatic retry and attempt).
- **Fetch now** is a normal button (no confirmation, nothing typed), shows "Fetching…" and can't be pressed again while running. When not allowed it is unavailable with the time and reason ("Available from 11:02. Fetches are at least an hour apart.", "An automatic retry is due then.", "The next scheduled fetch is due then.") or "Fetching starts when the event goes live" / "Inspection only"; the detailed skip reason, eligible time and lease-in-progress state are shown. ## 18. Audit and history
- Audit entries are immutable and commit atomically with their mutation. Each names the acting account, time, event, affected record, semantic action and useful previous/new values; rolled-back attempts are not changes.
- Routine history needs no typed reason. A written reason is required only where a rule says so: exceptional competitive/historical changes such as early end, correcting, rejecting or reversing evidence, and reopening official results (with strong confirmation).

Automatic successful WOM fetches go to WOM operational history. Provider request, confirmed outcome and unknown outcome stay distinct.
- Existing review, switch and domain history is reused instead of duplicate audit records: ReviewAction history is authoritative for decisions and rendered once in Audit, falling back to the audit entry for newer records without duplicates.
- Audit payloads, snapshots and request context never contain passwords, hashes, credentials, tokens, OAuth secrets, evidence credentials, signup-code values/hashes or unnecessary raw Discord IDs. Legacy reads are tolerant. One human-readable mapping serves full and recent views; raw safe details sit behind Technical details. Truthful historical labels are kept and missing historical names are never invented.
- Audit history is newest-first with fixed page size 25, filters for event, actor, action, entity and date, pagination that keeps filters and reaches all history, and readable before/after rendering. It cannot be edited, deleted or exported through any admin interface.
- Hiding an event is not an audit secrecy boundary: Admin Audit still shows hidden events under ordinary audit permission, with no suppression or repair; authorization, immutability and sensitive-data exclusion still apply.

## 20. Cross-cutting rules

### Authorization and validation
- Server authorization is authoritative for every page, form, fallback route, background action and realtime refresh. Every mutation rechecks the current account, event scope, role, lifecycle phase and expected version at the service boundary and repeats validation there. Client validation, rendered controls, links, notifications and route visibility grant nothing.
- Compatibility reads of retired data never keep retired commands authorized.

### Transactions and history
- Compound mutations commit authorization, validation, reservations, derived state, audit and required in-site notifications atomically. Reconciliation and retry re-read authoritative state first; a transaction failure is retryable and leaves no misleading success, partial reservation, role change or orphaned transition.
- Admin corrections identify the actor and preserve history; changes that alter competitive or historical truth also need strong confirmation and a written reason.
- Reversal, restoration, reopen, Resume, later evidence attempts and account recovery are explicit actions with their declared confirmation, reason and history.

### Failure feedback and recovery
- Every mutation reports its outcome on the authoritative page, distinguishing saved, already done/no change, field error, duplicate/conflict, stale edit, changed permission/lifecycle, dependency blocker, unavailable/rate-limited/incomplete provider and unexpected failure. Feedback says what happened, whether local state was saved and how to recover, without private data. A conflict names the field, record or blocker, keeps recoverable input and leaves committed state unchanged.
- Safe input is preserved. Severity and field errors never depend on English text or disappearing toasts. Unexpected failures use safe action-specific text and a diagnostic reference. Unknown upstream outcomes never claim nothing changed.
- A temporary external-service failure never rewrites event state; the user gets accurate unavailable/retry or manual-entry feedback.
- A stale confirmation is rejected and regenerated from fresh data, never applied to an altered schedule, role, board or lifecycle state.
- Every Admin fetch/JSON endpoint detects session loss (302, login HTML, enhanced navigation), keeps and shows unsaved input before sign-in, and distinguishes route refusal from an uncertain handler outcome; a 302 is not a 403.

### Admin attention
- Pending evidence and genuinely unresolved scheduled opening/start failures drive Admin actions and directory Needs attention; historical failures do not. There are no missing-Captain, vacancy or promotion follow-up actions. Personal read state resolves no business condition.

### Privacy
- Public projections contain only public event, roster, board, approved evidence, standings and participant-facing signup data. Usernames, Discord identity and security data, payment, Admin notes, audit, private evidence states and unnecessary account IDs are excluded.

### Routes and realtime
- Every protected journey has a real authenticated route and an ordinary server form/navigation path; JavaScript enhances it and does not define the operation. Public board, team, tile and approved-evidence destinations stay addressable; below 901 px ordinary route navigation replaces overlays; real URLs are the recovery path. No separate no-JavaScript parity journey is required.
- SignalR/realtime messages are invalidations: the page reloads authoritative state and handles stale versions. An invalidation never interrupts an active submission, confirmation, error or result state; ordinary reload stays available.

### Development reset
- Development reset creates only bounded labelled fixtures and minimal accounts, roles, states, teams, boards, signups, submissions and cached data (lookup event, Live competition cache, linked participant, Captain/Co-captain and Admin targets) without real data. It is idempotent and never calls Wise Old Man. Development navigation uses explicit event IDs/slugs from the reset output, never an arbitrary "current" event. A missing fixture is a setup limitation, never licence for a production shortcut.
- Production lifecycle services do not honour the Development fixture exemption, and it is unreachable through deployment input.

## 21. Non-functional requirements
- Integrity: approved progress is reproducible from the submission ledger; reversals restore the correct state; concurrent reviews never apply a submission twice; historical calculations use published snapshots.
- Security: public pages are read-only; captain authorization is server-enforced; passwords use a modern hash; uploads are validated and served safely; sensitive Admin actions are authorized and audited; hidden-event quarantine is Super Admin only, fails closed and has no public bypass; login and upload are rate-limited.
- Performance and scale: boards and team switching feel immediate; approval updates progress without manual refresh; large screenshots are resized for viewing while originals are kept; at least 100 simultaneous viewers; live updates avoid synchronized full-page reloads; WOM data is cached and fetched in the background, never per viewer request.
- Accessibility and browsers: current desktop and mobile browsers, keyboard-usable controls, reasonable contrast, meaningful alt text.

## 22. Out of scope and deferred
- Not in scope: multi-community hosting; Discord role/channel/message automation; automated prizes; RuneLite integration; automated drop verification; captain strategy tools, assignments or private notes; fully captain-operated or timed drafting (captain-controlled picks, automated turns, timers, alternative order systems, availability notes); automatic import as the only data path.
- No in-app feedback, bug or evidence-report form, persistence or API; reports go to the community Discord. A report is neither evidence nor an Admin action; valid evidence concerns enter normal review/reversal or a new submission.
- No toast Undo and no manual reordering of the waiting list or other queues. Display precision differences never justify changing stored EHB rounding.
- No automatic Captain status and no general cross-team Super Admin submission inspection or correction (the explicit read-only focus view is unaffected).
- Retired and not to be restored: emergency Captain authority, accountless creation, participant-owner transfer, Preformed restrictions, paused/reopened draft, delayed account switches, special Resubmit, Live replacements, manual result overrides, separate Archive action. Their retained data stays readable history only.
